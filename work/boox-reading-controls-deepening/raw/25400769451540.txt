You will learn how to adjust the layout of reflowable formats like EPUB, AZW3, DOC, DOCX, FB2, HTML, MOBI, RTF, TXT, etc. You can apply your favorite theme to any reflowable document with just one tap, saving time on repetitive setup.
Note: The UI of the current "Style" tab comes with Firmware V4.1.
 
Basic Settings
Here you can select different themes, adjust text size, change fonts, and choose a background color. [image:1] 
Advanced Settings
Tap "More Settings" or the selected theme again, so you can customize it further by finetuning font, spacing, margins, contrast, and others. In the latest Firmware V4.1, you can also copy these customized settings and apply them to other themes.
 [image:2] 
 
Font
Set different fonts for the title, body, and supplementary content, adjust the boldness of the text, enhance text color, and set character encoding.
 [image:3] 
Spacing
Tap the icon or drag the slider to adjust different spacing settings.
 [image:4] 
 
Margin
Drag the slider or input a value to adjust margins.
 [image:5] 
Contrast
Drag the slider or input a value to sharpen the images, bleach the watermarks, enhance dark colors, and improve the image quality.
 [image:6] 
Others
Here you can adjust the display of Chinese characters, control the indentation of paragraphs, change the alignment, switch text orientation, enable hyphenations, and choose the way pages are displayed.
 [image:7]
