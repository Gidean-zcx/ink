The Floating Toolbar is a useful feature that provides quick access to different functions in NeoReader. In this article, you will learn more about the Floating Toolbar in NeoReader.
 
Standard Floating Toolbar
The standard Floating Toolbar is displayed during reading.
 
 [image:1] 
 
To enable / disable or customize the Floating Toolbar, do the following:
In NeoReader, tap the center area to display the main menu.
Tap the hamburger menu in the top right corner and choose Settings > Floating Toolbar Settings > General Settings.
 
Here you can also add, remove or check the description of each button.
 
 [image:2] 
 
Handwriting Toolbar
The Handwriting Toolbar will be displayed in handwriting status. Please refer to Take Notes on Books page in the Reading chapter for more info.
 
 [image:3] 
 
To customize the Handwriting Toolbar, do the following:
In NeoReader, tap the center area to display the main menu.
Tap the hamburger menu in the top right corner and choose Settings > Floating Toolbar Settings > Handwriting Toolbar Settings.
 
Here you can also add, remove or check the description of each button.
