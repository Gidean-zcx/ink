The Status Bar is a useful tool at the bottom of NeoReader to display information, including time, battery value, remaining reading time, reading progress, and chapter name during reading.
 [image:1] 
 
 
To enable/disable or customize the Status Bar, do the following:
Tap any book file in the Library to open NeoReader.
Tap the center area to display the main menu.
Tap the More icon in the top right corner and choose Settings > Global Settings.
Toggle "Enable Reading Status Bar" and the settings below.
Drag to adjust the section order or tap to select which section is shown on the bar.
