You can set up the page-turn area in NeoReader based on your reading preference.
 
To set up the page-turn area, do the following:
Tap any book file in the Library to open NeoReader.
Tap the center area to display the main menu.
Tap the hamburger menu in the top right corner and choose Settings > Touch Settings.
Here you can choose one of the preset modes or customize your own settings.
