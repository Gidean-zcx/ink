Issue description
Meebook M7 is a 6.8-inch Android-based E-Ink reader with two physical page-turn buttons on the side of the device. The buttons are intended for navigating between pages while reading.
On my device, Android detects these buttons as:
Upper button: KEYCODE_PAGE_UP (92)
Lower button: KEYCODE_PAGE_DOWN (93)
The buttons work in other applications and are correctly detected by Android key event testing apps, but they do not trigger page turns in KOReader.
Regression
No
Last working version
No response
Steps to reproduce
Open any book (EPUB, for example).
Press either of the physical page-turn buttons on the side of the device.
Observe that nothing happens and the page does not turn.
