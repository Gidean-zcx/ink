Homepage 
Features
Britannica School: Early Elementary, 
crafted for Grades PreK–2, offers engaging, 
read-aloud articles with age-appropriate 
vocabulary, vibrant images, videos, and 
interactive features to keep young learners 
engaged. With easy navigation, students 
can dive into content made just for them!
Article and Media Browse
Students can explore available 
articles and media pieces through 
six categories. A read-aloud 
feature supports early readers, 
and exciting visuals make it easy 
for students to find what interests 
them most!
Search Bar
Support your students by 
helping them conduct a 
keyword search at the top 
of the homepage. Easily 
access the exact article 
or media piece, or view all 
results.

Articles
Explore low-Lexile range articles appropriate 
for young learners. Articles are sectioned 
and collapsible with less text to support 
emerging readers. Students can listen to 
articles read aloud, cite the article, and share 
through various platforms. They can also use 
the English/Spanish toggle to translate all 
media content into native Spanish.
English/Spanish Toggle
An easy toggle between English 
and Spanish boosts language 
acquisition and accessibility, 
seamlessly translating articles 
and all related media into  
native Spanish.
Easy-to-Use Navigator
Effortlessly print, cite in four styles (MLA, 
APA, Chicago, and Harvard), adjust 
the font size for readability, and share 
articles directly to Google Classroom, 
Microsoft Teams, or Google Drive for 
seamless classroom integration.
Text Support Features
The quick-click dictionary allows 
you to quickly find definitions and 
Spanish translations for any word. A 
read-aloud feature with word-by-
word highlighting is available via the 
play button at the top of each section, 
supporting emerging readers.
Explore the Catalog
Our article database grows weekly! Discover our 
full collection.

Britannica Fundamentals
If your subscription has Britannica Fundamentals enabled, it will inspire emergent learners 
to read, explore, play, and openly create in a vibrant environment. Set the tone for a day of 
exploration with colorfully illustrated stories, captivating images and videos, interactive maps, 
and a fun digital art board.
Read
Animated e-books introduce 
fundamental concepts and build 
language and comprehension skills. 
Topics range from animals and colors 
to places and shapes, with narrated, 
word-by-word highlighted text.
Explore
In the explore section students 
can browse seven global biomes 
and see where they’re located on 
the interactive map.
Play
Interactive games build early 
literacy and math skills with 
multiple-choice and drag-
and-drop questions guided by 
animated characters!
Create
Illustrate concepts or have fun 
drawing freely with brushes, colors, 
stickers, and backgrounds. Use the 
eraser, reset, and print options to 
customize and save creations.

Teach Britannica: 
Resources for Educators
Teach Britannica is an educator’s companion, offering ready-to-use resources that support 
instruction and enrich classroom learning for Early Elementary students. Through this 
dedicated hub, educators can access tools like graphic organizers, instructional strategies, 
and lesson minis—all designed to make teaching easier and more impactful.
Accessing  
Teach Britannica
Teach Britannica is easily accessible 
from the Britannica Education icon 
in the main navigation on Britannica 
School: Early Elementary.
Lesson Minis
These modular activities are designed 
to promote personalized learning and 
boost engagement. These minis include 
options for differentiation, variations, 
and collaborative teaching—ideal for 
lesson planning, sub plans, and real-
world extension activities.
Instructional Strategies
Access seven categories of evidence-
based methods for effective education, 
such as Acquire Vocabulary and 
Organize Thinking, which are essential 
for learning goals of any classroom.
Graphic Organizers
Visual tools such as diagrams, charts, 
and structured visuals support 
student understanding of relationships, 
processes, and concepts, making 
complex ideas easier to grasp.
