Kindle Personal Document File Types That Support Notes
Discover which Send to Kindle file types are compatible with Kindle formats and notes for your Kindle E-Reader and Kindle applications.
Note: You can write directly on the page in PDF documents on Kindle Scribe and stylus-supported Android and iOS devices or write on the page in other personal documents imported through the
Send to Kindle service. Handwriting isn't supported in PDF documents loaded via USB-C or previously sent to your library before 11/11/2022.
Handwriting features aren't supported in EPUB comics, Manga, and children's books currently.
