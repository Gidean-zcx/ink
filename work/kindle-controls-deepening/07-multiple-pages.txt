Manage Multiple Notebook Pages on Kindle Scribe
To multiselect notebook pages and perform bulk actions, use Select in the top toolbar of the notebooks overview. You can also long-press on notebook pages.
To open notebook overview, select the multipage icon  in the top toolbar. While in multiselect view, all the pages that you select show a checkmark in their top-left corner.
To add a page after the selected page, select Add in the top toolbar. If you have multiple pages selected, this feature adds a page after every selected page. You can also add a page after the last notebook page using the large plus page-like icon .
To delete selected pages, use Delete in the top toolbar.
To move selected pages, use Move. To place them, select the vertical lines between any two pages.
To share selected pages, use Share. You can choose to share the currently selected pages, or the entire notebook.
You can share your handwritten notes as is. The email sent contains a PDF with your notes attached.
You can also convert your handwritten notes to text and share them. The email sent contains a TXT file, along with a PDF of your handwritten notes.
On Kindle Scribe models released in 2025 and later:
To create a duplicate of any selected page, use Duplicate in the top toolbar.
To move a page or a copy of a page to another notebook, use Move in the top toolbar.
To exit multiselect view, use the
X in the top-right corner to go back to notebook overview.
