Use Quick Notes on Kindle Scribe
Access Quick Notes from Home and jot down your ideas, thoughts, daily drivers, and sticky reminders.
Note: To use this feature on your Kindle Scribe:
You need a Kindle Scribe released in 2025 or later.
You need to register your Kindle Scribe to an Amazon account and connect it to the internet.
Quick notes are a type of notebook pinned to your Kindle Scribe Home. It displays the last page used in the notebook.
You can resize the Quick notes on Home by tapping the double chevrons ">>" or "<<."
Tapping on the plus icon  next to or under Quick notes adds a new page to the end of the Quick notes notebook.
Within Quick notes, you can move or copy pages to another notebook by using Move in the top toolbar. You can also delete pages.
You can find the Quick notes notebook in Workspace.
