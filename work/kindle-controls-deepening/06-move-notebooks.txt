Move a Notebook on Kindle Scribe
Books and personal documents get stored separately and it isn’t possible to move them into notebook folders.
Make sure that you have created a folder where to move your notebook.
Tap the 3-dot menu below a notebook cover.
Tap on the Move option and follow the steps to select the folder where you want to place your notebook.
To move a notebook to the Notebook level tab, select a notebook that's in a folder and follow the steps described. Select Notebook tab instead of a folder.
