Create a Folder or Notebook on Kindle Scribe
Kindle Scribe allows you to create notebooks for you to jot down your thoughts, take notes, or create To-do lists. Notebooks get auto-saved when you close them.
You can organize your notebooks using folders on the Notebook tab. Your device must have at least 100 MB of available storage at all times to be able to create and save new folders and notebooks or new strokes on notebooks.
Tap the Notebook icon next to the Search Toolbar in the upper right corner of the screen.
Alternatively, in the Notebook tab, tap the + icon in the upper right corner of the screen to create a notebook or create a folder.
If these steps don't solve your issue, press and hold the Kindle power button to prompt the restart menu and restart the device.
