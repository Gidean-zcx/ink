Write an opinion piece
O
ne way to express your opinion is to write about it.  
In an opinion article, a writer explains why their view 
is one that other people should consider agreeing with. 
Many newspapers have a section for these articles (often 
called op-eds), or they may publish letters to the editor 
from readers. Your school newspaper may also publish 
opinion pieces. Here’s how to write your own.
Choose a subject that’s personal to you
If you write about something that affects you 
personally, you’ll have more to say about it. 
Think about events happening in your 
community or school. For example, 
maybe there is a proposal to upgrade 
your town’s playground but some 
residents oppose the cost. Check out the 
box at right for a list of topics you may 
have a strong opinion about. 
Support your perspective
First, state your opinion. Then give two or three facts 
that support your viewpoint. Use your own experience 
to strengthen it. For example, you might write, “I think 
a new playground is necessary because many kids in 
the community have gotten hurt on the current 
equipment.” Notice the inclusion of the word 
“because.” Using “because” in your article allows you to 
clearly state the evidence that supports your opinion. 
Research the other side
In journalism, a reporter presents the facts without 
including their opinion (known as being objective). For 
an opinion article, a writer doesn’t have to present 
both sides of a topic. However, showing that you are 
aware of opposing views and including them in your 
article could make it stronger. You might 
write, “I know a new playground will  
cost the town money. However, I still 
believe it will benefit us because safe 
playgrounds support healthy activity.” 
End with a call to action
Once you’ve made your supporting 
points in your opinion article, give 
readers a next step, known as a call to 
action. You could write something like 
“We must act together now” and provide the date  
of the next town meeting when the playground 
upgrade will be discussed. Ask an adult to proofread 
your article, then submit it to your school or local 
newspaper. Even if your work isn’t published, writing 
it is a great way to practice expressing your opinion. 
Find out how you can persuade others to understand your point of view.
