Host a book trivia night
A 
book trivia night is a fun competition in which 
participants see how many questions they can 
answer about books, authors, characters, and plots. It’s 
also a great way to find out about new books you may 
want to read! Here’s how you can plan a book trivia 
night with your family or friends. 
Set a theme
If you’d like, you can select a theme for your book trivia 
night. For example, the theme could be 
“legendary series,” “fantasy favorites,”  
or “historical fiction.” If you and your 
friends love a particular popular series, 
such as Harry Potter or The Baby-Sitters 
Club, you could also build a trivia night 
around those books. (For more book 
suggestions, turn to “The 50 Books  
Kids Love Most” on page 12.)
Come up with the questions
Ask your guests to think of five trivia questions 
to write down on paper, sticky notes, or index cards. 
They should include the answers, but be sure no one 
else can see them! They can be about characters, the 
plot (avoid spoilers!), authors, or memorable lines. For 
example, a question could be, Which book has this 
sentence as its first line: “Once there were four children 
whose names were Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy.” 
(The answer: The Lion, the Witch, and the Wardrobe.) For 
more question inspiration, see the box at right.
Prepare to host
Choose a comfortable spot for everyone to gather, with 
chairs and throw pillows for guests to sit on. Put out 
pens and paper. Hang up drawings of famous book 
characters if you like, and arrange some snacks, 
such as bowls of gummy worms for your 
“bookworm” guests, on a table. 
Run the game 
Now it’s time to play! You can play 
individually or set up small teams of 
two or three players with fun names 
like the Page Turners or the Mystery 
Solvers. Have everyone take turns reading 
the questions they brought, and give 
players a minute to write down their answer. Then 
everyone can reveal their answers together. The goal 
is to correctly answer as many trivia questions as 
possible. Ask a volunteer to keep score to determine 
a winner! If you like, you could also end your trivia 
night with a used book swap (see opposite page).
Have fun connecting with friends and family over your shared love of reading.
Here are a few ideas to kick off 
a book trivia night! How many 
of these would you get right?
● Which Hogwarts house is 
Harry assigned to in Harry 
Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone?
Answer: Gryffindor 
● Which book is about a robot 
who becomes shipwrecked on 
a remote island?
Answer: The Wild Robot
● Which popular children’s 
book author drew a comic strip 
for his college newspaper? 
Answer: Jeff Kinney
● Whose life does Charlotte 
save in Charlotte’s Web?
Answer: Her friend Wilbur’s
