Search for a string of text within the document that is currently being displayed on the Digital Paper.
Tap anywhere on the screen of a document to display the tool bar.
Tap  (search) in the upper right of the document screen.
Tap the [Search Text] tab — the search term input field. Then use the keyboard to enter a string of text, and tap [Search].
Tap one of the search results.
The page that you tapped will be displayed.
To close the search results list
Tap an area outside of the search screen.
Note
There is no differentiation between upper case and lower case letters.
Handwritten text and text that is embedded in an image cannot be searched.
