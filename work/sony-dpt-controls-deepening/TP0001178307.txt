Change the settings so that annotations that were made to a document outside of your Digital Paper cannot be edited.
Press the
(home) button, and tap [Settings].
Tap [System Settings] — [Limit Editing Annotations] — [Enable].
Note
The handwritten sticky notes, text sticky notes, and bookmarks that were added to a document using the former model, “DPT-S1,” cannot be edited regardless of the [Limit Editing Annotations] setting.
