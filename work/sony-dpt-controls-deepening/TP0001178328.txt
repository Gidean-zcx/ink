You can search for a page by displaying a list of 16 pages on a single screen.
Tap anywhere on the screen of a document to display the tool bar.
Tap  (option) in the upper right of the document screen — [Page Thumbnails].
To return the display to normal size
Tap any one of the pages.
Hint
When switching from documents that are being displayed side-by-side, nine pages are displayed in the list.
When you pinch in on the screen while displaying a document, the page thumbnails can be displayed.
