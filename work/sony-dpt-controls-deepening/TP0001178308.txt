Handwritten annotations and highlighted sections can be displayed in a list and searched for in pages.
Tap anywhere on the screen of a document to display the tool bar.
Tap  (option) in the upper right of the document screen —  [Annotation List].
Tap the [Handwriting List] tab or the [Highlight List] tab.
Tap one item from the list.
The page that you tapped will be displayed.
To close the annotation list
Tap an area outside of the annotation list.
Note
Depending on the number of annotations within a document, it may take time for the annotation list to appear.
