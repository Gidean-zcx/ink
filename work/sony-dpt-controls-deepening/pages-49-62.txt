Set the way you hold the pen, and correct any displacement between where the pen touches the screen and where the

From the four variations of how to hold the pen, select the variation that most resembles your own grip,

Use the pen to trace the dotted lines as you would normally write.
A line does not appear atop the dotted line as you trace. When you lift the pen, the line appears.

on the confirmation screen will be displayed, so correct the placement again, as you did in step 6. After you finish
correcting the position, tap [Next], and trace the dotted line on the confirmation screen again.
8
When the dotted line and the line that you drew appear to line up, tap [Save].
