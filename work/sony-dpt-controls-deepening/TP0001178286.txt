When [Limit Editing Annotations] is set to [Enable], annotations that were made outside of your Digital Paper cannot be edited. To make these annotations editable, press the  (home) button and tap [Settings] — [System Settings], then change [Limit Editing Annotations] to [Disable].
The handwritten sticky notes, text sticky notes, and bookmarks that were added to a document using the former model, “DPT-S1,” cannot be edited.
When the battery level of the pen is low, you may not be able to edit or delete annotations. Charge the pen.
When the document is protected, you cannot edit or delete annotations.
When there is limited free space in system storage, documents will open as “Read-Only,” and you cannot edit or delete annotations. Delete unnecessary documents to increase the amount of free space.
When operating the Digital Paper using the Digital Paper App, you temporarily may not be able to edit or delete annotations. Wait until operations are complete.
