Annotations cannot be made if the pen battery is too low. Please charge the pen.
Annotations cannot be made while a document is being saved.
When there is limited free space in system storage, documents will open as “Read-Only,” and annotations cannot be made. Delete unnecessary documents to increase the amount of free space.
Highlights cannot be added to a document that was scanned (pages containing only image data) or on text that is embedded in an image.
When the Digital Paper is being operated via the Digital Paper App, annotations may be temporarily unavailable. Wait until operations are complete.
