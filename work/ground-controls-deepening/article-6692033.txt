Link Multiple Sign-In Options To Your Account
Edited Wednesday, June 10 2026
You can now link multiple sign-in methods to your Ground News account directly from the website. This gives you more flexibility and convenience when accessing your account, without changing your primary email. By connecting additional sign-in methods, you can use social login options such as Google, Apple, or Facebook for quick and secure access.
How to Manage Your Sign-In Methods
Visit your Ground News account page on the website.
Go to the “Sign-in methods” section under the Account Information tab.
From there, you’ll be able to:
View which sign-in methods are currently connected.
Add new sign-in methods.
Remove a connected sign-in method, if needed.
(Click on the image to expand)
Important Notes
Please keep in mind:
You can only link one account per provider (e.g., one Google account, one Apple ID, etc.).
Your account data, saved articles, and subscription details remain unchanged, no matter which sign-in method you use.
Removing a sign-in method does not delete your account.
If you experience any issues, please reach out to our support team at feedback@ground.news, and we’ll be happy to assist. We appreciate you for being an active and engaged member of the Ground News community and our team is here to help ensure a smooth login experience!
