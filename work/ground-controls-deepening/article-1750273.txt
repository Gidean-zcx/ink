How to Submit a News Source to Ground News
Edited Tuesday, October 13 2026
Ground News features coverage from over 60,000 news sources worldwide, and we're always looking for opportunities to expand our coverage. Whether you're a news organization interested in featuring your articles on Ground News or a reader recommending a news outlet, we'd love to hear from you!
For News Organizations: Submit Your News Source
If you represent a news organization and would like your content to be considered for inclusion on Ground News, please complete our News Source Submission Form.
Step 1
Visit our News Source Submission Form.
Step 2
Complete the form with your news organization's information, including your website URL and any additional details requested. Note: We encourage you to provide as much relevant information as possible to help our team review your submission.
Step 3
Submit the form. Your request will be forwarded to the appropriate team for review and consideration. Note: While all submissions are reviewed, we cannot guarantee that a news source will be added or provide a specific timeframe for inclusion.
For Readers: Recommend a News Source
Is there a news outlet you'd like to see featured on Ground News? Send an email to feedback@ground.news with the name and direct link of the news outlet you'd like to recommend, so our team can identify and review the source. We'd love to hear your suggestions. If the source is already available on Ground News, we can also help point you in the right direction.
Looking for additional support? Let our team help you.
If you have questions, our team is here to help. You can contact us at feedback@ground.news or +1 (866) 471-9181, and we'd be happy to support you.
PS - We're always working to make the Ground News Help Center more helpful for our community. If you have suggestions for improving this article or notice something that could be clarified, we welcome your feedback at feedback@ground.news.
