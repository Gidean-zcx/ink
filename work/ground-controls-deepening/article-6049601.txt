Does Ground News Have A Comprehensive List of Featured Sources?
Edited Wednesday, March 18 2026
Ground News gathers news from over 60,000+ sources globally, offering a wide range of perspectives across regions, political leanings, and media types. Because our database is constantly growing and evolving, we do not provide a static or downloadable list of all sources.
Can I Access a Full List of Sources?
We do not offer a full or static list of all sources on Ground News, since our coverage is continuously expanding. Instead, you can search for and follow specific sources directly within the platform. Learn more on how to search for news sources or coverage that matter most to you here.
How to Search and Follow News Sources
You can easily check if a particular source is available on Ground News using our search feature.
Ground News Website
Use the search bar near the top-right corner of the page and search for the name of a news source
If available, click on the source title or logo to visit our source page for more details or select the Follow Icon (+) to add the source to your For You page
Ground News Mobile App
Tap the Discover tab at the bottom of your screen to search for the name of a news source
If available, click on the source title or logo to visit the source page for more details or select the three dot ellipsis to follow and add the source to your For You page
Following sources helps personalize your feed and ensures you see content from outlets you care about. Learn more about following your favorite topics and interests here.
Ground News Browser Extension
You can quickly access coverage for any article or news source that you come across online by using the Ground News Browser Extension. Once installed, the extension adds a “Full Coverage” button to supported source pages across the web, including news sites and social media platforms.
When you're on a news story and select the "Full Coverage" button on the Browser Extension, you’ll be taken directly to the story’s page on Ground News, where you can:
Compare coverage from different outlets
View bias and factuality ratings, as well as ownership data
Explore multiple perspectives on the same story
Our Browser Extension is available on Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, and Opera, and you can learn more about this works here.
