Current Known Issues (Expanded)
Bug: "Edit Bias Rating" Error on Some Android Devices
We’re currently investigating an issue affecting some Android readers where they are unable to edit the Bias Rating for certain sources using our "Edit Bias Rating" feature.
Temporary Fix
If you're using our Android mobile app, you can still edit the Bias Rating for a news source:
From the news source topic page, if a rating already exists
From a story page, whether or not a rating already exists
Current Status
Our team is investigating this issue and working on a fix.
Bug: "Invalid Login" Error When Using Google Sign-In
We've investigated an issue affecting some readers where attempting to log in using Google Sign-in results in an error message that says, "Invalid Login"
Temporary Fix
If you encounter this issue, we recommend deleting and reinstalling the app, then attempting to sign in with Google again. If the issue persists after reinstalling, please reach out to our team at feedback@ground.news , and we will generate a temporary password for you to sign into your account while we investigate further.
Current Status
Our team is actively investigating and working on a fix.
Bug: Mobile App Not Loading on Some IOS and Android Devices
We’re currently investigating an issue affecting some readers where the Ground News app does not load properly on launch. The screenshot below shows the error message “Something went wrong” that may appear when the app fails to load.
Temporary Fix
If you encounter this issue, try reinstalling the app. To do this:
Delete the Ground News app from your device.
Reinstall the Ground News app from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.
Log back into your account.
This usually resolves the error message and restores normal app performance.
Current Status
Our team is investigating this issue and working on a fix.
Bug: Wrong Headline Appearing on Mobile Apps
We’re aware of an issue affecting the Compact Card mode on the Ground News iOS and Android Mobile App, where some readers may see a mismatch between the headline displayed on the Story Card and the main story’s headline in the Newsroom. Our development team has identified the cause and is actively working on a fix.
Temporary Fix
To avoid this issue in the meantime, you can turn off Compact Card mode by following these steps:
Open the Ground News app.
Navigate to Profile.
Tap on Settings.
Locate Compact Cards and toggle the setting off.
Current Status
Our team is investigating this issue and working on a fix.
Still Having Trouble?
If the issue continues after following these steps, our support team may be able to help investigate further. You can contact our support team at feedback@ground.news for assistance and we'd be happy to help.
