My Bias Bar Colors Are Wrong
Edited Saturday, April 11 2026
If the colors on the Ground News bias bar look different from what you expected, it is usually because your Ground News edition or account settings are set to a different regional color scheme.
Different countries use different political color associations and the Ground News bias bar is designed to represent the political party associations, depending on your Ground News edition. In countries such as the UK, Canada, and Australia, the left-leaning parties are associated with red, and the right-leaning parties are associated with blue, so the colors are set accordingly in our UK, International, and Canadian Editions.
Follow the steps below to confirm or change your bias bar color scheme.
How to Fix the Issue
Check Your Ground News Edition (Web Readers)
The bias bar colors change depending on which Ground News edition you are currently using. To confirm or change your edition on the website:
Sign into your account on our website and select the edition drop-down menu in the top-right corner of the page
Select the edition you'd like to opt into for your account
Changing the edition for your account will update the bias bar colors depending on the region.
For a full walkthrough on changing your edition, visit: Follow Relevant News For Your Region
Set Up Your Account Preferences (Web Readers)
If you'd like to change the color scheme of the bias bar without having to change the Ground News edition, you can set up your Account Preferences to the US or International color scheme. To set this up on the website:
Sign into your account on our website
Select My Account at the top right-hand corner of the screen
Select Preferences on the selection bar on the left-hand side of the screen
Click on the Bias Bar drop-down and select the US or International color scheme
This will change your bias bar color scheme while keeping your preferred Ground News edition!
Change the Bias Bar Color Scheme (Mobile App Readers)
If you are using the Ground News mobile app, you can manually choose your preferred bias bar color scheme, regardless of the edition you are viewing.
To update the color scheme on the app:
Launch the Ground News app
Tap the Profile tab (bottom-right corner of the page)
Tap the Settings icon (top-right corner of the page)
Select Bias Bar Color Scheme
Choose your preferred scheme (US or International)
Still Having Trouble?
If the issue continues after following these steps, our support team may be able to help investigate further. You can contact our support team at feedback@ground.news for assistance and we'd be happy to help.
