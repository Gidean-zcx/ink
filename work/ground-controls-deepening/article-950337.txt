Follow Positive & Uplifting News
Edited Monday, March 9 2026
In a world filled with serious news, finding moments of joy, positivity, or even innovation can help you maintain a balanced perspective. If you find yourself wanting to add some good news in the daily news cycle or simply want to discover new, uplifting stories, this Help Center Article covers how to navigate and customize your news feeds to discover a more balanced and positive news experience.
How to Find Good News, Offbeat Stories, and Other Uplifting Interests
Ground News offers a variety of interests that cater to different tastes and preferences. If you're looking for a weekly palate cleanser, feel free to check out and follow the links below:
Join us every Wednesday as we curate a collection of stories that will be sent directly to your inbox to either make you smile, surprise you, or perhaps leave you questioning the ordinary by signing up for the Offbeat weekly newsletter.
Are you looking for heartwarming and feel-good news? Check out and follow our Wholesome Interest page.
Discover uplifting stories about advancements and positive changes in various fields by exploring the Innovation interest page.
For those intrigued and delighted by the wonders of the natural world, we have a topic page dedicated to Science and Nature.
Dive right in and explore the latest in technology and stay ahead of the curve with our Tech interest page.
Looking for more uplifting content? If you find yourself wanting to explore and follow other topics or interests in your feed, we have a dedicated help article addressing this specific feature.
Exploring Personalized Feeds
Often, Top News (consisting of important news stories covered by many sources) or Blindspots can be dominated by political narratives. If you're looking to break up your news consumption and explore more about what's happening in the world outside of the political sphere, we've got you covered!
Your For You Page
Find coverage made for you on your For You page. The content you find here is determined by your location, followed topics/sources, and app usage patterns. It includes local news updates and "My Feed", where you can view your personalized news feed. We recommend searching for a topic by entering a word or phrase in the search bar, and follow it by clicking the plus sign symbol to follow it.
Custom Feeds*
Pro+ subscribers now have the power to create personalized news feeds focused on select topics or locations that matter most to you. This enhancement goes beyond the capabilities of My Feed, allowing you to curate mini-feeds that align perfectly with your interests.
*This feature is currently only available on Ground News web, but will be coming to the apps shortly.
If you'd like to learn more about Custom Feeds, rest assured as we have a help article which speaks to this feature.
Show Me Less of a Topic
Encountered a topic you'd rather not see on your feed anymore? Whenever you come across a story you’re not interested in, tap on the three dots in the top right corner of a story and choose "Show me less content like this." This will inform the algorithm to show you fewer articles about that topic and you should start seeing changes in your newsfeed immediately.
