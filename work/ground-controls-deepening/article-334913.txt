I Want To Delete My Account Data
Edited Tuesday, July 28 2026
To delete your Ground News data, please follow the steps below:
First, log out of the platform on all of your devices. You can sign out via the Account screen on the Ground News website, and via the Settings screen in the Ground News app (at the bottom of the menu).
Fill out the data deletion form here.
Important Reminder
Filling out a data deletion your account's data, but it will not affect your subscription status. If you would like to cancel your subscription, please refer to this article. Upon submitting the Data Deletion form, you will sign the declaration statement at the bottom acknowledging that Data Deletion does not affect your subscription status.
