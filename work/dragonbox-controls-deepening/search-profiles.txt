# How to create, edit, and delete Kahoot! Kids profiles

Updated September 17, 2026 13:59

In Kahoot! Kids app, you can create child profiles that are individual spaces where each young Kahoot!’er can save and track their progress.

The free version of Kahoot! Kids includes one child profile. The ability to create multiple profiles is included in certain plans. 

If an additional profile is not included in your current plan and you try to add it, you’ll be redirected to the relevant plan within the app.  You can upgrade directly there!

## How to create a child profile

  1. After accessing Kahoot! Kids, tap on the avatar icon at the top of the screen.
Image
  2. Tap on the plus icon - Add.
Image
  3. Provide your age. It must indicate that you’re an adult.
  4. Provide the child’s age.
  5. Set a nickname.
  6. Select the avatar.
  7. Done!
Image

## How to edit or delete a child profile

### In the Kahoot! Kids app

  1. Open the Kahoot! Kids app.
  2. Tap on the Parents icon in the top left corner of the screen.
Image
 
  3. Provide your age. It must indicate that you’re an adult.
  4. Click on Profiles button
  5. To edit or delete a child profile tap on its avatar and click on pencil icon in the settings section.
  6. Save the changes provided.
Image

### In the Kahoot! app

  1. Go to the settings by tapping on your avatar in the top left corner.
  2. Select and activate the adult profile.
☝️ You won't be able to delete child profiles when a child profile is selected as the current active profile.
  3. Tap and hold on to the child profile’s avatar. 
  4. Tap Edit profile.
  5. Edit name or age and tap Save.
  6. Alternatively, select the Delete Profile option at the bottom of the screen.
Image

# Child Activity Dashboard: your parent dashboard for Kahoot! Kids

Updated October 17, 2026 04:06

The Child Activity Dashboard is a parent dashboard in the Kahoot! app and the Kahoot! Kids app. It shows how your child is learning across all self-study experiences in Kahoot!, all in one place.
Wondering how much time your little Kahoot!'er spent learning this week, or which game to play next? The dashboard brings together their solo kahoots, Quiz Games, Math Games and learning apps into a single overview. You'll see their activity over the last two weeks, their lifetime progress, and tips on what to play next.

Prefer a quick update in your inbox? You can also sign up for a free monthly email report with a summary of your child's activity.

## Who can use the Child Activity Dashboard

The dashboard shows activity for child profiles and young students. Every parent can open it, and your plan decides how much detail you see.

What you see  | Free  | Kahoot! Kids subscription or Kahoot!+ Bronze and above
Total learning time  | Yes  | Yes
Completed Activities: accuracy, sessions and time per game  | No  | Yes
Topic Progress  | No  | Yes
Boost Learning recommendations  | No  | Yes
Monthly email report  | Yes  | Yes

## How to open the Child Activity Dashboard

If you haven't added a child profile yet, you can do it from your Profile page or while setting up your account. See How to create, edit, and delete Kahoot! Kids profiles.

You'll find the dashboard on the Profile page in both the Kahoot! app and the Kahoot! Kids app.

  1. Open the Kahoot! app or the Kahoot! Kids app.
  2. Tap your profile icon.
  3. Scroll down and tap Child Activity Dashboard. 
Image
  4. To see another child's activity, tap the profile picture in the top-right corner and choose their profile.
Image
  5. Done! Scroll down to see their activity, progress and recommendations.

## What you'll see in the dashboard

The dashboard shows your child's activity across kahoots, Quiz Games, Math Games (including Math Missions) and learning apps. Use the This week and Last week tabs to switch between weeks, and tap the profile picture in the top-right corner to switch to another child. The time under the title shows when the data was last updated.

  * Weekly overview
  * Completed Activities
  * Topic Progress
  * Boost Learning

#### Weekly overview

See your child's total time spent in the selected week and how it compares with the week before (for example, 4h 32m, up 17%), their most played topic, and how many minutes they learned each day from Monday to Sunday. A purple flame badge replaces the day's letter on every day your child learned, so you can see at a glance how consistent their learning habit is. Days without activity keep their usual letter, and today has a grey outline until your child completes an activity.

#### Completed Activities

See every activity your child finished that week, labeled with its topic. The result shown depends on the type of activity:

Image

#### Topic Progress

See how much time your child has spent on each topic over their lifetime, such as Elementary Math, Literacy and Future-ready Skills. Under each topic, you'll see how many kahoots they've played and a progress bar for each learning app or Math Game. Games they haven't tried yet show a Start button. Tap it to preview the game before you hand the device to your child. Tap View all to see every topic.

#### Boost Learning

Get tips on what to play next. Depending on your child's activity, the dashboard can suggest continuing a game they've already started (like Learn to Read), trying something new (like Duck Duck Fractions), playing kahoots on a topic they're learning (like Multiplication with Kahoot!), or creating a quiz for your child yourself. Tap Go to jump straight in. To see how your child moves through Math Games, read Math Missions: How to play and progress.

## Monthly email report: your child's progress report in your inbox

The monthly email report is a free summary of your child's learning activity over the past month. It arrives during the first week of every month. Use it as a quick check-in, then open the full dashboard for deeper insights or to discover new games to play together.

### What's in the monthly report

Each report covers one calendar month, for example January 1–31, and is personalized with your child's name.

Weekly learning time

See how much time your child spent learning each week. The weekly chart helps you understand their learning routine and identify changes throughout the month.

Monthly learning total

Find out how much time your child spent learning during the month, including an equivalent number of school days.

Achievements by learning area

Explore your child's activity across different learning areas, including:

  * Kahoots played
  * Practice sessions completed
  * Challenges attempted

Recommended next step

Based on your child's monthly learning progress, the email may suggest another learning app to explore. Select Open to view the recommendation.

Explore more in the Activity Dashboard

Want to learn more about your child's activity? Select Open Activity Dashboard in the email to view additional learning insights.

### How to sign up

  1. Create a Kahoot! account with your email address, or log in if you already have one.
  2. Go to your Profile page and tap on Child Activity Dashboard.
  3. Tap on Opt into emails. 
Image
  4. Done! Your first report arrives in the first week of next month.

## FAQ

  1. Does Kahoot! have a parent dashboard? Yes. The Child Activity Dashboard is Kahoot!'s parent dashboard. It's available in the Kahoot! app and the Kahoot! Kids app, under Settings.
 
  2. Is the Child Activity Dashboard free? Yes, every parent can see their child's total learning time for free. Detailed insights like accuracy, topics and recommendations need a Kahoot! Kids subscription or a Kahoot!+ Bronze plan or higher.
 
  3. Can I track my child's learning across all Kahoot! games? Yes. The dashboard combines solo kahoots, Quiz Games, Math Games and learning apps into one overview. It's the first Kahoot! report to bring all of these together.
 
  4. How far back does the dashboard go? You can switch between this week and last week. Topic Progress shows your child's lifetime progress, and Math Missions practice sets and assessments appear as kahoots with their percentage of correct answers.
 
  5. How often is the data updated? Time spent is updated daily.
 
  6. When is the monthly email report sent? Reports are sent during the first week of every month. They cover your child's activity over the previous month.
 
  7. Do I need a subscription for the monthly email report? No, the monthly report is free. You just need a Kahoot! account with your email address.
 
  8. Who does the dashboard show activity for? It shows activity for child profiles and young students. If you have several child profiles, you can view each child separately.
