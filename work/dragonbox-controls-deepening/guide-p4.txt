The sandbox
The sandbox allows your child to play freely with the Nooms.
It’s the perfect place for kids to get to know the Nooms, and for teachers and 
parents to explain different mathematical concepts. It is designed so that 
kids, parents and teachers can come up with their own problems to solve 
and experiments to perform.
