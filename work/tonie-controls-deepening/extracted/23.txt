#### FAQs

## Tonieplay Questions & Answers

##### What do I need in order to play a Tonieplay game?

To play a Tonieplay Game you’ll need a Toniebox 2 and a Tonieplay Controller. If it’s the first time the game is being played you’ll also need a WiFi connection, but after this it will work offline too.

##### Is Tonieplay compatible with Toniebox 1?

No, because of the tech advancements of Tonieplay it will only work on Toniebox 2.

##### What aged children is Tonieplay suitable for?

There are Tonieplay games for everyone from 3 years and up, with more coming all the time.

##### How much help will my child need with playing a Tonieplay game?

Tonieplay Games are designed to be easy to follow, but we’ve also included a written manual in every box for those who prefer to read up first. Younger players may need a little support at the beginning – but they’ll be playing independently in no time.

##### Are Tonieplay games multiplayer?

All our Tonieplay Games are designed for solo play but can also be enjoyed together.

##### How do Tonieplay games appear in the app?

Once you’ve loaded a Tonieplay Game on the Toniebox 2 you’ll be able to see it in your collection section of the app, just like Tonies

##### Can I skip past the intro to the Tonieplay game?

Yep. Most games include a skip feature so you can move forward or back through the story. Intros are usually skippable, but some moments – like answering a quiz question or making a choice in an interactive story – can’t be replayed right away.
