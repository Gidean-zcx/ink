# A Creative Tonie does not appear in the Toniecloud account

If a Creative Tonie is not listed in the Toniecloud account after it has been placed on the Toniebox, it has most likely already been registered in another household.

A Creative Tonie containing audio content is permanently linked to the Tonie household in which it was connected to the content. He cannot move to another Toniecloud account together with the content. If the Creative Tonie should change its Toniecloud account, either the content must be deleted, or the Creative Tonie must be removed from the previous Toniecloud household. Both options are only available to the owner of the original Tonie household or full members in that household.

#### Useful tips:

For example, if you have been given a Creative Tonie with content as a gift and want to edit or add to the playlist, it is recommended to get invited as a full or Creative Tonie member to the Tonie household where the Creative Tonie is located. The Creative Tonie will appear in your Toniecloud account after a successful invitation.
