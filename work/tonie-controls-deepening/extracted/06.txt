# Meet the Toniebox 2: Features FAQ

# MEET THE TONIEBOX 2!

Image: 1.gif

#### Below we break down all the new features of Toniebox 2, just click and explore:

FULL BATTERY DISPLAY

The Toniebox 2 is built to keep up with busy days, snoozy nights and hours of songs, stories and fun.

When you plug in the USB-C Charging cable, the Dynamic Light Ring will visually show the battery level.

Image: 2.png

Want to check remotely? Simply head to the tonies® app while your Toniebox 2 is connected to Wi-Fi to view and monitor the current battery state.

SLEEP TIMER WITH LIGHT

The Sleep Timer with Light lets you choose when the Toniebox powers down, gently fading out the sound and glow to help your little snoozer drift off peacefully.

Head to the tonies® app to set your desired time, the sleep light will then turn on and the Tonie placed on top of the Toniebox 2 will play until the timer runs out, before going to sleep too.

Image: 3.jpeg

SUNRISE ALARM

Image: 4.gif

The Sunrise Alarm eases them into the day with a gentle sunrise glow and wake-up tunes.

Simply head to the tonies® app each night before bedtime and set your desired wakeup time for your little listener.

DYNAMIC LIGHT RING

The Light Ring glows in rainbow colours when Tonies are playing, and gives helpful light cues to guide kids through the Tonieplay games.

Image: 5.gif

TONIEPLAY

Tonieplay unlocks new world of interactive games, stories, quizzes, challenges and more, exclusive to Toniebox 2. 

Ready to play? Here’s how:
1. Pick a Tonieplay game.
2. Put it on top of the Toniebox - first play? The Tonieplay game will need to be downloaded before it can be play. This will typically take up to 30 minutes (depending on your internet speed). Note that our longer games, like Quizzles, will take the longest time to download.
3. Add the Tonieplay controller in the middle of the light ring and press the button to start.
4. Get ready to play! 

Image: 6.gif

WORKS WITH ALL TONIES

You heard right! Every Tonie that has been released prior to launch of Toniebox 2 will work with the Toniebox 2, and all future Tonies will work with the Toniebox 1. 

Please note: Tonieplay Games will not work with Toniebox 1.

Image: 7.pngImage: 8.pngImage: 9.png

CERTIFIED 1+

The Toniebox 2 is certified 1+ 

Designed and tested to be safe, intuitive, and engaging for kids aged 1 and up.

The all new 1+ or 3+ mode can be set and changed via the app. To ensure you have control of the content that can be played on your Toniebox 2.

3+ Age Mode: 🎵 Can play while charging.

1+ Age Mode: ⚠️ Cannot play while charging and tilt to rewind or fast-forward is disabled.

Image: 10.png

USB-C CHARGING 

Power up in no time – keep your Toniebox 2 fully charged and ready to play with fast and easy USB-C charging.

You can use the 1+ 20cm charging cable included with your Toniebox 2, or any other third party compatible USB-C charging cable. 

Image: 11.pngImage: 12.png

NEW TONIES® APP CONTROLS

With the tonies® app, you can check the battery level, manage your Tonies collection and tweak settings right from your phone. 

And there's more to come – fresh features are being added all the time to allow you to co-pilot your little one's listening experience.

Image: 13.png

PLAYBACK CONTROLS

👀 Live Playback Status: See exactly which Tonie is on the box and view the chapter currently playing, right from your screen.

⏯️ Pause and Play: Halt the action for a quick snack break and jump right back in when you're ready.

⏮️ Restart and Skip: Instantly rewind to a favourite song or skip ahead to the next chapter.
