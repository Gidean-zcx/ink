### Why does my Toniebox 2 not play a Tonie when it is plugged in?

This is expected behaviour when your Toniebox 2 is set to 1+ mode. If you want to change this setting, simply open the tonies® app, tap on your Toniebox 2, select settings and the scroll to "Age Mode" to change from 1+ to 3+.

### Tonie is playing the audio from a different Tonie

If the Toniebox 2 starts playing audio from another Tonie, don’t worry—your little one has likely just swapped their Tonies too quickly. This is an easy fix: simply remove the Tonie from the Toniebox 2, wait a few seconds, and then place the desired Tonie back on top to resume the listening fun.

### Tapping the Toniebox 2 to skip forward and back isn't working

If your Toniebox 2 isn’t skipping tracks when you give it a tap, don’t worry—it may just need to turn off and back on again. Your Toniebox 2 will turn off automatically on its own after five minutes of no activity. When it wakes up again, the tapping magic should be back in action.
