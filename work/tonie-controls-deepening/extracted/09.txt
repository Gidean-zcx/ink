L18: # How long does the Toniebox 2 remember your auto-saved status when you remove the disc, take a break or play something else?
L19: If you remove your Tonieplay disc for a break or to play another Tonie product, the progress will stop. This means that the game is not "paused" anymore, but you will load it up from scratch again.
L20: However, each game acts a little differently. For example the quiz games will not continue a running round of let's say 5 questions. An interactive story will be resumable at the last position. A Discover Game will start a new "round".
