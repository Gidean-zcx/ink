L18: # What new Tonieplay specific data will we capture?
L19: When you use the new function Tonieplay, usage and operation information about the in-game interaction with the Toniebox will be collected. We process the data (e.g. frequency of use of individual games, errors that resulted in termination of use) for the same purpose as we process all other Interaction Data of the Toniebox.
L20: Please find more information about processing Interaction Data in our Privacy Notice in Section “Data Usage, Toniebox, Tonies, mytonies and the Tonies Account” paragraph 3 and the following.
