L18: # Can I skip past the intro to the Tonieplay game?
L19: 
L20: Yes! Most games include a skip feature so you can move forward or back through the story. Intros are usually skippable, but some moments – like answering a quiz question or making a choice in an interactive story – can’t be replayed right away.
