L18: # What do I need in order to play a Tonieplay game?
L19: 
L20: To play a Tonieplay Game you’ll need a Toniebox 2 and a Tonieplay Controller.
L21: 
L22: If it’s the first time the game is being played you’ll also need a WiFi connection, but after this it will work offline too.
