L18: # Can I restart a Tonieplay game?
L19: 
L20: Need to go back a bit in your game?
L21: 
L22: Easy! Simply lift the Tonieplay Disc off your Toniebox 2, count to five (a little dramatic pause never hurt anyone ✨), and pop it back on. Your game will restart from the top, or depending on the Tonieplay game, will pick up at your most recent checkpoint, if you've reached one. Think of it as closing a book and opening it again to your bookmarked page...
L23: 
L24: A few things to know:
L25:   * A full reset is not possible, until the game is completed so keep on playing.
L26:   * No checkpoint yet? The game will begin from the very start of your adventure.
L27:   * Reached a checkpoint? Taking the disc off and back on will restart the game to that checkpoint.
L28:   * Mid-adventure and want a quick breather? Removing the disc is a safe way to pause and step away; just pop it back on when you're ready to continue.
