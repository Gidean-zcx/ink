If you’re not sure which eReader you have, follow the steps here.
Before you start:
Note: Third-party Bluetooth page turners are not officially supported on Kobo eReaders. Some devices may work, but compatibility and functionality can vary and cannot be guaranteed. Verify that your headphones or speaker has built-in Bluetooth.
On most Bluetooth-enabled products, you’ll see the logo or Bluetooth icon on the device or box it came in.
Place your Bluetooth headphones or speaker close to your
eReader.
Make sure your Bluetooth headphones or speaker is fully charged.
Ensure that you don't have other devices paired to your Bluetooth
speaker or headphone.
Step 1: Set your Bluetooth headphones or speaker to pairing mode
Ensure your Bluetooth headphones or speaker is in pairing mode. When
your headphones or speaker is in pairing mode, it will let your eReader
connect to it.
For example, before you can connect your Airpods to your Kobo Sage, you’ll
need to set your Airpods to pairing mode.
For support on how to set your headphones or speaker to pairing
mode, follow the instructions that came with your device.
In some cases, you may have to unpair your Bluetooth headphones
or speaker before you can pair it with your eReader.
If your Bluetooth headphones or speaker was previously connected
to another device (for example, your laptop), you’ll have
to first unpair your headphones from your device. After your
headphones are unpaired, you can then set it to pairing mode
so that it can connect to your eReader.
Pairing tips
Ensure that your Bluetooth headphones or speaker are: Nearby your eReader.
In pairing mode.
Fully charged.
Away from other WiFi devices such as your cellphone
or tablet.
Step 2: Scan for Bluetooth devices on your eReader
On your Kobo eReader, Bluetooth requires Wi-Fi to be on. When you enable
Bluetooth, Wi-Fi will automatically turn on.
Follow the steps in this article to pair and troubleshoot Bluetooth devices
on your eReader:
Connect your Bluetooth headphones or speaker to your Kobo eReader
If you're still unable to see your Bluetooth device on your eReader:
Turn your eReader's Bluetooth off and then back on.
Turn your Bluetooth headphones or speaker off and then back
on. Next, set it to pairing mode so that it’s discoverable
by your Kobo eReader.
Rescan for available Bluetooth devices on your eReader
You can rescan for your Bluetooth devices within the audiobook player
or using the Settings menu.
Rescan within the audiobook player menu
With your audiobook player open:
Tap No Bluetooth Connected near the
bottom of the screen.
Tap Rescan .
Tap on the name of your Bluetooth headphones or speaker to
connect to it.
Rescan within the Settings menu
Go to your Home screen .
Tap More at the bottom of the screen.
Tap Settings .
Tap Bluetooth Connection .
Beside ‘Bluetooth’, tap the circle icon to turn Bluetooth on. After Bluetooth is enabled on your eReader, set your Bluetooth
headphones or speaker to pairing mode.
Tap Rescan at the bottom of the screen. Your
Bluetooth headphones or device should appear in the list.
Tap on the name of your Bluetooth headphones or speaker to
connect to it.
Step 3: Remove your Bluetooth device from the connection list
If the steps above didn't work, try removing the Bluetooth headphones
or speakers from your Bluetooth connection list, and then pair your Bluetooth
headphones or speakers to your eReader.
Before you start:
To reduce signal interference, keep your phone and other
WiFi devices away from your eReader and Bluetooth headphones
or speakers.

Tap Settings .

Tap Forget .
