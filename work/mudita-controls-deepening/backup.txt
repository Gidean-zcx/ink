 How To Create and Restore Mudita Kompakt Data Backups With Mudita Center
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:45 AM
How to create a backup for Kompakt
Open Mudita Center and connect your device 
Look under Your Kompakt > Click Overview
Click Create backup
Click Create backup to confirm
Create a password to protect your backup data* 
Click Confirm passcode
Enter your passcode to create the backup
*Warning! You can not change the password after you confirm it.                    
 If you don’t want a password, click Skip password to create the backup 
How to restore Kompakt from a backup
Open Mudita Center and connect your device 
Look under Your Kompakt > Click Overview
Click Restore from backup
Select the Backup you want to restore from the list
Click Restore from backup
Enter the password protecting the backup* 
Click Confirm 
*Warning! There is no way to recover or change the password for backups.             
 If no password was created, you will skip this step and start the restore process
How to find the backup file to restore Kompakt
Open Mudita Center and connect your device 
Look under Mudita Center > Click Settings*
You will see the file path (Example: C:/User/Documents/Mudita/Kompakt/backup)
Follow the file path using your computer’s browser
*The backup tab opens by default, if it does not, click Backup at the top of the screen
