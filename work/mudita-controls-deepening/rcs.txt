 Does Mudita Kompakt Support RCS Messaging?
 Modified on: Fri, 31 Jul, 2026 at 6:40 AM
No, Mudita Kompakt does not support Rich Communication Services (RCS). We do not plan to introduce RCS support for this device.
Mudita Kompakt uses standard SMS and MMS for messaging to ensure a simple and reliable communication experience.
