 How To Manage Volume Levels on Mudita Kompakt
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:45 AM
How to change the volume level
There are 2 ways to change the main volume level on Kompakt but you can also change the levels for certain functions like the alarm or ringtone too.
To set the main volume though the Control Center:
Press the Control Center button
Swipe the sound volume bar below the Bell icon up or down
To set the main volume using the volume buttons:
Press or hold down the volume up button to raise volume
Press or hold down the volume down button to lower volume
To change the volume for certain functions:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Sound
Use the sliders to set the level you want
How to put Kompakt in silent mode
There are 2 main ways to put Kompakt into silent mode.
To set silent mode though the Control Center:
Press the Control Center button
Tap the Bell icon or swipe the sound volume bar all the way down
To set silent mode using the volume button:
Hold down the volume down button until the sound volume bar is all the way down
How to turn certain sounds on or off 
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Sound
Tap Advance
Use the toggle to turn on or off sounds for: 
Dial pad presses
Screen locking
Charging (includes vibration)
Touch sounds (button presses etc.)
