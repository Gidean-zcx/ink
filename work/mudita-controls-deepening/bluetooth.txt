 How To Connect Devices Using Bluetooth to Mudita Kompakt
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
Before starting, ensure the device you want to connect to has Bluetooth turned on and is discoverable.
To connect a device:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Connected Devices
Tap Pair new device
If you see a notification “Bluetooth is on”, tap x to dismiss it
Select the device you want to pair with from the list
Tap Pair
