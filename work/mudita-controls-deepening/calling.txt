 How To Make Calls and Manage Emergency Number on Mudita Kompakt
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:44 AM
How to make a call on Kompakt
To call a contact: 
Tap Phone
Tap Contacts
Select the contact you want to call
Tap Call
To call a new number: 
Tap Phone
Tap Dial
Enter the number you want to call
Tap the Call icon
How to block a number from an incoming call
When you receive a call from the number you want to block: 
Tap the Block icon
Tap Block number
How to change the emergency call number
Warning! Your emergency call may not be answered if you change the default number!
The default emergency number is for the emergency services in your state or country.
To change the emergency number:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Safety and Emergency
Tap Emergency Call
Tap Call in case of Emergency 
Tap the existing number
Enter the new number
Tap Save
