 How to Unhide or Hide App Icons on Mudita Kompakt
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
Some app icons like weather, calendar and alarm are hidden by default, but you can unhide them if you prefer.
To unhide or hide apps: 
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap App layout
Use the Toggle switches to unhide or hide the apps*
Tap the Back arrow icon to save the changes and exit
*To see how your app screen will look, tap Preview
