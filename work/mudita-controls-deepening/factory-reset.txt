 How To Do a Factory Reset on Mudita Kompakt
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
Warning! This will delete everything on your phone! Your MuditaOS K version will stay the same.
To do a factory reset: 
Press the Control Center button
Tap the Settings icon
Tap System
Tap Factory Reset
Tap Factory Reset
If you are sure you want to delete all your data, tap Factory Reset
Kompakt will restart and be ready to set up
