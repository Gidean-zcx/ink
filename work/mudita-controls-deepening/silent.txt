 How to Put Kompakt in Silent Mode?
 Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:35 AM
There are two main ways to enable silent mode on Kompakt
Method 1: Using the Control Panel
Open Control Center.
Tap the Bell icon or slide the volume bar all the way down to mute the device.
Method 2: Using Settings
Open Settings.
Go to Sound.
Turn on Silent Mode.
