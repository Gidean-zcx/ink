Check the availability & requirements of Connected Apps in Gemini Apps
The availability of Connected Apps vary by location, language, device, and Gemini app. For example, a connected app might be available on the Gemini mobile app but not on the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com, or the other way around.
The table below lists the following details about Connected Apps:
The connected app and description: The name of the connected app in Gemini and a few examples of what you can do with it in Gemini Apps.
An app’s availability and requirements:
Account types: The type of Google Account you must sign in to Gemini Apps with to use the connected app.
Ages: The age requirement to use the connected app.
Locations: The countries and regions where the connected app is available.
Languages: The supported languages for the connected app.
Subscriptions: The required Google AI subscription, if any, to use the connected app.
The supported Gemini Apps & experiences: The Gemini Apps and experiences (such as chats, tasks, and Gemini Live) where you can use the connected app.
