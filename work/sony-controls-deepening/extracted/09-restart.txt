Restarting the player
Restart the player if operations become unstable.
Restarting will not delete any data and pairing information.
Hold down the (power) button ( [1] ) for 2 seconds while the screen is turned on.
Tap [Restart].
Hint
If the player freezes, hold down the (power) button for 10 seconds to force the player to restart.
