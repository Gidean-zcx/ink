1. These settings affect behaviour of the app for the local

2. These settings have no impact on the connected Shanling
   device. ﻿





                          USB Exclusive mode

                                      Setting enabling for direct output from the app to the USB DACs,
                                    avoiding any system processing.
                                     Also compatible with headphones with USB-C connector.

                                            1. Go to "Playback Settings" > "USB Output".

                                            2. Turn on the "USB Exclusive mode". Adjust other setting
                                  depending on your needs and your USB DAC.

                                            3. Connect the USB DAC and confirm the pop-up window,
                                       allowing the app to access the DAC.
