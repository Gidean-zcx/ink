NAS

Function allowing app to access audio files stored on local NAS
servers.

1. Go to " Cloud Music" > "NAS".

2. App will search for available local servers, click on the server
  to be accessed. If your server is password protected, you will
  be asked to input your password at this step.



                                 Bluetooth Headphone AMP Control

                                     Various devices can be added to the app to access their settings,
                                   usable mainly with Bluetooth Amplifier devices.

                                            1. Go to " Device control" and choose " Bluetooth headphone
                           amp control".

                                            2. Pick corresponding category of device and click on it, it will
                                      search for all compatible devices. Click on the device to
                                      access it.





DLNA

Eddict Player app can act as a DMC for DLNA compatible devices.
App finds content on digital media servers (DMS) and instruct
digital media renderers (DMR) to play the content. Allowing app
to control playback and volume.

Connecting:

1. Make sure your streamer is connected to Wi-Fi.

2. Please make sure your device supports DLNA and that it's
  enabled.

3. In Eddict Player, click on the   DLNA icon in Playback interface.

4. Select your Streamer to act as a DMR to play the file.
