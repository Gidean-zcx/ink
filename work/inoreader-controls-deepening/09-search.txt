Find any article in your feeds and beyond
Everything you follow is searchable, as far back as it was collected. With Pro, you can also search millions of public sources and turn any search into a feed of new matches.

Search everything you collected

Search scopes

Precise queries

Frequently asked questions
What does search cover?
Search covers everything in your account, including your feeds, folders, tags, saved articles, annotations, uploaded files and Intelligence reports, as far back as the articles were collected. With Pro, it also covers every public feed in Inoreader, whether you follow it or not.
How do I search inside one feed or folder?
Open the feed or folder and use the search bar at the top. The results are limited to that feed or folder. On the Search page, you can pick the scope from a list instead.
Can I search feeds I do not follow?
Yes, with Pro. Choose all public feeds, or one of the featured collections of trusted sources on a topic, and the search runs across millions of articles from the sources Inoreader indexes.
Which operators and filters are there?
You can search for exact phrases in quotes, use AND, OR and NOT, and choose whether to search the title or the content. There are also filters for language, time period, match type and order. The query builder offers the same options in a form. In it, you can add a company, product, place or person as a mention instead of a keyword, so a search for Apple the company does not bring back the fruit.
Can I get new results for a search automatically?
Yes. Click the button next to the search bar to turn the search into a monitoring feed. You do not have to run the search again. Every new article that matches your query arrives in the feed as it is published, and the feed sits in your account like any other.
Do the apps search too?
Yes. The iOS and Android apps search your account and, with Pro, all public feeds, with the same scopes and filters.
