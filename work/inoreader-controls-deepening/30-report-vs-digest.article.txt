Should I use an Intelligence report or an email digest?
Use an Intelligence report for analysis across several articles, such as a comparison or an overview of recurring themes. Use an email digest to send a curated selection of content to recipients on a schedule. Digests can also include AI summaries. Choose based on whether your main goal is to analyze material or distribute selected content.
