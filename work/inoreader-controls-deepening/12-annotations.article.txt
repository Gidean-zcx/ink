Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between a note and a highlight?
A note belongs to the whole article, and you can add one to any article on every plan. A highlight marks a passage inside the article, and with Pro you can also write a note on that passage.
How do I highlight a passage?
Select the text while you read and click Highlight text. The passage stays marked in the article and also appears in the Annotations panel at the top, where you can add a note to it.
Where do I find my annotations later?
Open Saved and then Annotations. Every article you highlighted or wrote a note on is listed there, and each one opens with its annotations at the top.
Can I search my notes?
Yes. Your notes are indexed together with the article, so a search for a word you wrote in a note finds the article it belongs to.
What happens to a highlight if the publisher updates the article?
Your highlight stays where you put it. When you highlight an article, Inoreader keeps your own copy of the text you highlighted, so a later change on the publisher's side does not move or remove it.
Can I send my highlights to other tools?
Yes. Connect Readwise under Preferences > Share, save, login, and your highlights and their notes are sent there as you make them. Your annotations also work in the iOS and Android apps.
