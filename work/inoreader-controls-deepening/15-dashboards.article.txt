Build your dashboard
Open Dashboards from the sidebar. The default dashboard shows your most read feeds, trending articles and recommended sources.
Click the button at the top right to add a widget, then use its gear icon to choose what it shows, such as a folder, a feed, a tag or a monitoring feed.
Drag the widgets into place and choose a layout. If one dashboard is not enough, add another with the plus in the tabs.
Open the dashboard you use most, click the three-dot menu at the top right and choose Set as start page.

Frequently asked questions
What goes on a dashboard?
Widgets. They can show the new articles from all your feeds, a folder or one feed, what is new in your most read feeds, trending articles and the latest results of a monitoring feed. Other widgets list the articles you recently tagged, saved or read, show your reading statistics and unread counts, recommend sources, list failing feeds or bring you the latest from the Inoreader team.
Can I have more than one?
Yes. You can make a dashboard for each way you use Inoreader, for example one for work and one for leisure, each with its own widgets and layout. You switch between them from the tabs at the top.
Can I change the layout?
Yes. Choose one, two or three columns, drag the widgets where you want them, and use the same widget several times for different folders.
Can a dashboard be my start page?
Yes. Any dashboard can be the page Inoreader opens to, both on the web and in the apps.
Is there a dashboard for a team?
Yes. On Team plans, admins build shared dashboards that every member sees, with widgets for Team folders, channels, tags, uploads, reports and digests.
Is it free?
Yes. Dashboards and their widgets are available on every plan. Team dashboards need a Team plan.
