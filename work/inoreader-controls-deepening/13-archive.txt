Frequently asked questions
How far back does a feed go?
A feed's articles are kept from the day Inoreader first saw the feed. For most feeds, that is years before you subscribed, because someone else was already following them. New articles are added as they are published, and nothing is dropped because it is old.
Can I read articles the feed no longer lists?
Yes. A publisher's feed usually holds the last ten or twenty items, but Inoreader holds everything it has collected, so you can read the feed's history by scrolling or with search.
Is the archive searchable?
Yes. Search covers every article of your feeds, whatever its age, by keyword, phrase and operators. On Pro, filters narrow the results by time and language.
Do I need to save articles to keep them?
No. Every article of the feeds you follow stays in Inoreader whether you read it or not. Save or tag an article only when you want it in your own collection, for example so that it stays with you after you unfollow its feed.
Can I take it with me?
Yes. On Pro, you can export any folder or tag as an output feed. You can also save articles as PDF and export your subscriptions as OPML at any time.
If I unfollow a feed, does it keep its history?
The articles stay in Inoreader as long as the feed has readers, and if you follow it again, its history is back. Articles you tagged or saved stay in your account either way.
