Frequently asked questions
What is a spotlight?
A spotlight is a named group of words or phrases with a color of its own. Wherever one of those terms appears in Inoreader, in a list of articles or inside an article, it is shown in that color.
How do I create one?
Open Automate, then Spotlights, and click Create spotlight. Give it a name and, if you like, a description, pick one of ten colors and list the terms you want marked.
Does a spotlight hide or remove articles?
No. A spotlight only colors the terms it contains and changes nothing about which articles you see. To keep or remove articles by keyword, use a filter.
Can a term match only with the exact capitalization?
Yes. Each term can be set to match case, which helps with short names and acronyms that are also ordinary words.
How do Team spotlights work?
Team admins can make a spotlight visible to all Team members. Each member decides whether to show Team spotlights, and when a term is in both a personal and a Team spotlight, the Team spotlight's color is used.
Is there a limit?
No. Spotlights are free on every plan, and you can create as many as you need and turn each one on or off at any time.
