      Direct Your Device with a Wave of the Hand

      Navigate on your tablet with a few simple hand gestures.

      Try it out

      Notification Panel: Swipe down from the upper left edge of the screen to view and manage app notifications.

      Control Panel: Swipe down from the upper right edge of the screen to display control panel, to quickly enable or disable frequently-used features.

      Gestures: Go to > System & updates > System navigation and select Gestures. You'll then be able to use the following hand gestures:

      • Back: Swipe inward from the left or right edge.

      • Return to the home screen: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen.

      • Access recent tasks: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and hold for a short while.
