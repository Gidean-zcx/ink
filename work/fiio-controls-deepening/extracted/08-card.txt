23. How to insert/eject the SD card to JM21?
1.The JM21 is compatible with micro SD card.
2.Insert the SD card (Connecting fingers facing downward ) to the SD card slot.
3.Please poke the bottom of the card in order to lock it in the player.
4.You could poke the bottom of the card to eject the SD card.
