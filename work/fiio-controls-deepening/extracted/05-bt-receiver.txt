8. How to use the JM21 as a Bluetooth receiver?
1. Go to the drop-down or Settings menu of the JM21, and choose the working mode as Bluetooth receiving mode.
2. Go to the Bluetooth transmitting list of the mobile phone, and tap the JM21 to complete the connection.
If the connection fails, it is recommended to turn off the Bluetooth of mobile phone and JM21, then turn on the Bluetooth function again for pairing and connection.
