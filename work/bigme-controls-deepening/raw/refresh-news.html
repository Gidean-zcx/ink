Bigme's Massive Update: Experience the 80FPS Revolution on Hibreak Pro Color, Hibreak Dual & B7 Pro

June 11, 2026

If you have been following the evolution of E-ink technology, you already know the biggest historical trade-off: giving up smooth performance in exchange for eye comfort and battery life. But what if you didn't have to choose anymore?

In a groundbreaking move for the best E-ink smartphones of 2026, Bigme has just rolled out a massive update. The Hibreak Pro Color, Hibreak Dual, and the B7 Pro series are officially getting their refresh rates boosted to a blistering 80FPS.

Let's dive into what this hyper-fast update means for your daily workflow and why this is the biggest leap forward for the color E-ink tablet and smartphone market.

🚀 The 80FPS Breakthrough: Why It Matters

For years, users have searched for ways to reduce ghosting and input lag on E-ink displays. Bigme's self-developed xRapid super refresh technology and Auto Ghosting Elimination algorithms have always led the pack, but hitting 80 Frames Per Second entirely shatters the ceiling.

With the 80FPS update, you can expect:

  • Zero-Lag Typing: Send emails, text, and take notes at your normal speed without waiting for the screen to catch up to your thumbs.
  • Fluid Scrolling: Browsing social media, reading long web articles, and navigating the open Android 14 OS feels incredibly smooth and natural.
  • Dynamic Media: You can comfortably watch informational videos directly on the E-ink display with virtually unnoticeable ghosting.

This isn't just an incremental patch; it's a paradigm shift for anyone looking to reclaim their screen time without sacrificing modern smartphone performance.

📱 Hibreak Pro Color: The Pocket Powerhouse

The Bigme Hibreak Pro Color has already turned heads as the ultimate distraction-free device for modern professionals. Powered by the robust Dimensity 1080 octa-core CPU, the Pro Color model now utilizes the 80FPS update to its full potential.

Whether you are using its 6.13-inch Kaleido 3 screen to review documents, or utilizing the 5G global connectivity for on-the-go research, the ultra-fast frame rate ensures your productivity is never interrupted.

🔀 Hibreak Dual: The Best of Both Worlds Just Got Faster

If you are looking for a definitive Bigme Hibreak Dual review, the verdict is in: the world's first E-ink + LCD dual-screen smartphone is a game-changer. It was designed to give you an eye-soothing E-ink main screen for 95% of your day, and a vibrant LCD sub-screen (360x360 resolution) for when you need pinpoint color accuracy.

Now, backed by the 80FPS E-ink refresh rate:

  • Using the native stylus on the Bigme Infinity Canvas feels exactly like writing on real paper.
  • The unique "One-Key Cast" feature transitions media between the E-ink and LCD screens flawlessly.
  • Multitasking across its massive 12GB RAM and 256GB storage is snappier than ever.

Want to see what the community is saying? Check out the buzz in the Reddit E-ink community to see how users are reacting to this unprecedented speed.

💼 B7 Pro Series: Peak Productivity

If you prefer a larger canvas for your work, the Bigme B7 Pro Phone-Tablet is your go-to. Known for hitting an impressive AnTuTu score of 720K, the B7 Pro pairs its recent core hardware upgrade with this new 80FPS capability.

Featuring AI-powered smart call summarization, worldwide 4G calling, and a borderless handwriting experience, the 80FPS update solidifies the B7 Pro as the ultimate tool for reading, annotating, and managing your digital life without eye strain.

🌍 The Future of E-ink is Here

Bigme continues to push the boundaries of what E-ink devices can do. By bridging the gap between traditional glowing screens and eye-friendly E-readers, the 80FPS update ensures that you get top-tier Android 14 E-ink performance alongside incredible battery life.
