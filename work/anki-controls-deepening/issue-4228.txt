Secondly, I would also love for the auto advance feature to continue to advance when the Anki window is not in focus. As it is now, it automatically disables when a different window is in focus, which is unfortunate.
Originally reported on
https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/auto-advance-features/64549
The current behaviour was chosen to minimize the chances of the user accidentally answering all their cards when they get distracted and switch away from the app, but as they require a menu/shortcut key to start auto advance, maybe it's reasonable to assume they'll know how to stop it too.

I agree with this 100% i actually was just searching for how to do this. if the concern is people accidentally accepting cards, make the auto advance intentionally opt in. for instance, maybe the only way to have auto advance is to make a specific type of deck that only auto advances, and does not count towards your goals.
my specific use case, is i just want to get reps in, i have copied my main deck, and am just playing the word audio and the words sentence, and this auto advance deck i do not care about the answer as its not my main deck.  i do this during work, and would love to be able to do other things without auto advance stopping.
i think the ability to create a completely disconnected deck that auto-advances is a valid feature request.
