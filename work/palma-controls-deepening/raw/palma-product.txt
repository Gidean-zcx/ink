[1] BOOX Super Refresh Technology (BSR) is an exclusive innovation unveiled by BOOX in 2022 to improve the refresh performance and image quality of its ePaper products. The BSR technology with four refresh modes can make the image run smoothly or reduce ghosting.
[2] The dark mode in the NeoReader is available with the BOOX firmware above version 3.5.3. Users can switch between white and dark mode in NeoReader's main menu.
[3] E Ink ComfortGaze™ Front Lights offer a comfortable viewing experience day and night. Users can adjust the screen brightness and color temperature to the level they feel most comfortable with.
[4] The type C interface of Palma 2 supports OTG (On the Go), allowing users to connect a USB flash drive and other peripherals to the device.
[5] BOOXDrop is a feature built into BOOX Android devices. Users can activate the BOOXDrop and use the LAN (Local Area Network) to perform two-way transfers quickly between BOOX and computer and mobile devices.
