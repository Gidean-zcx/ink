In this article, you will learn how to use different tools like BOOXDrop and the send2boox website to transfer files and webpages.
Transfer Files
Transfer Files with BOOXDrop (Recommended)
1. Connect your BOOX device and your computer with the same LAN (local area network).
2. Open the BOOXDrop app on your device and enter the URL in your computer browser.
3. Here you can send files to your BOOX device or download files to your computer.
4. You can check the file in
the Receive tab
in the BOOXDrop app on your device.
Transfer Files with send2boox Website
1. Enter the send2boox websites the same as that on your BOOXDrop App into your computer browser. (If you want to change the server, click
here
.)
US/VN Server:
https://push.boox.com/
EUR Server:
https://eur.boox.com/
2. Log into your account.
3. Click the
BOOXDrop
tab, where you can send files and
screensaver
to your device in the
Online transfer
, and you can check the file in the
Push tab
in the BOOXDrop app on your device.
4. You can also do a two-way transfer by clicking the device in the
Local transfer
. Ensure that your computer and your BOOX device connect to
the same LAN
and choose
the same
server
.
Transfer Webpages
Transfer Webpages with send2boox Website
1. Enter the send2boox websites the same as that on your BOOXDrop App in your computer browser. (If you want to change the server, click
here
.)
US/VN Server:
https://push.boox.com/
EUR Server:
https://eur.boox.com/
2. Log into your account.
3. Click the
PushRead
tab and then choose
Webpage
.
4. Enter the
URL
of the webpage and click
Add
.
5. Check the webpage on the
All tab
or
ONXY Push
tab on the
PushRead
app
of your BOOX device.
