Navigate your tablets through Screen Rotation, Navigation Bar, Gestures, and Navigation Ball.
Screen Rotation
The screen of all models can be rotated. If your device is designed with an asymmetric bezel, you can rotate the screen to place the slim side to your dominant hand so the wide side can be held by your non-dominant hand. Auto-rotate is only available for models with G Sensor.
1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the Control Center.
2. Tap the Rotate button in the Shortcut Switches.
3. Choose the preferred orientation in Manual rotation.
Smart Assistant
Smart Assistant is a screen of useful shortcuts on your BOOX Tab Series device.
How to Access
Swipe right on the first page of the Home Screen to access Smart Assistant.

System Bar
The System Bar is an informative area displayed on the top of your device to show
time, Bluetooth connection, battery power, etc
.
Control Center
Control Center can be opened by swiping down from / tapping the right side of the System Bar. This area contains some quick settings for your devices. You can also customize the Shortcut Switches area by tapping the Edit icon in the upper right corner.
Notification Center
The Notification Center can be opened by swiping down from / tapping the left side of the System Bar. This area displays notifications from your device and apps. You can also manage notifications or perform Clear All by tapping the recycle bin button from the top right corner.
Navigation Bar
Use the navigation bar on the bottom to get quick access to common functions. Do the following to enable and customize the Navigation Bar:
Settings＞System Navigation＞Navigation Bar＞More Settings
Note
: In some apps where the Navigation Bar is automatically hidden, you will need to swipe up from the bottom to call out the Navigation Bar first.
Gestures
Basic Gestures
Tap:
To select an item or choose an option.
Swipe Left:
To go to the next page.
Swipe Right:
To go to the previous page.
Three-finger Swipe Down
: You can take a screenshot by swiping down the screen with three fingers. To enable or disable the gesture, go to
Settings > More Settings > Screenshots
.
Long Press:
To open a pop-up menu or check the properties of files.
Bottom Gestures
Swipe up in different parts from the bottom to perform quick actions. You can customize it by doing the following:
Settings＞System Navigation＞Bottom Navigation＞More Settings
Side Gestures
Side gestures are turned off by default. You can enable and customize them by doing the following:
Settings＞System Navigation＞Side Gestures
1. Up-and-down Swipe Gestures
Swipe up or down on either side of the border to adjust the volume, front light, or contrast settings.
2. Left-and-right Swipe Gestures
This part of gestures is turned off by default. You can enable and customize them to perform going back or other actions by swiping inwards on either side of the border.

Navigation Ball
The Navigation Ball is a system feature that provides quick access to different functions. You can customize up to 9 functions on the Navigation Ball.
Enable / Disable the Navigation Ball
There are several ways to turn on / off the Navigation Ball.
Method 1: Via the Navigation Ball App
1. In Home Screen, find and open the
Navigation Ball app
.
2. Turn on / off "
Enable Navigation Ball
".
Method 2: Via the Settings page
1. In Home Screen, go to
Settings > System Settings > Navigation Ball
.
2. Turn on / off "
Enable Navigation Ball
".
Method 3: Via the Control Center
1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the
Control Center
.
2. Find and tap the
Navigation Ball button
in the Shortcut Switches to turn it on or off.
Note
: If you cannot find the Navigation Ball button on the Control Center, you may do the following:
1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the
Control Center
.
2. Tap the
Edit
icon.
3. Drag and drop the
Navigation Ball
button to the enabled Shortcut Switches.
Navigation Ball Gestures
Tap
: Expand or collapse the navigation menu.
Double Tap

Drag
: Move the Navigation Ball.
