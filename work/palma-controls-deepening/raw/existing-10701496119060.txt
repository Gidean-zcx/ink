In this article, you will learn more about the functions of the Contents tab in NeoReader.
To access the Contents tab, do the following:
In NeoReader, tap the center area to display the main menu.
Tap the
Contents
tab.
TOC (Table of Contents)
Here you can check and directly jump to a specific chapter or section with the built-in TOC of the book file.
Bookmarks
Here you can check, manage or locate your bookmarks in the book.
To add a bookmark, simply tap the icon on the top right corner of the page. It will be indicated by the hollow bookmark icon turning solid.
Annotations
Here you can check, manage or locate your annotations in the book, which include your highlighted, underlined or annotated content.
To learn how to make annotations, please refer to the
Take Notes on Books
page in the Reading chapter.
Handwriting
Here you can check, manage or locate your in-book handwritten notes.
To learn how to make in-book handwritten notes, please refer to the
Take Notes on Books
page in the Reading chapter.
