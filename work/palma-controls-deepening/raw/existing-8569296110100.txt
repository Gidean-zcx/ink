In this article, you will learn several methods to take notes on books in NeoReader.
Highlights, Underlines & Annotations
Firmware V4.1 simplifies the
Annotation Toolbar
, allowing users to customize the icons on the toolbar. To make highlights, underlines, or annotations, do the following:
1. Long-press the content to select it, and the
Annotation Toolbar
will be displayed. You can choose the style and color of lines and highlights.
2. 2. You can mark content by underlining or highlighting. The content you marked will be shown in
Contents
>
Annotation
.
To manage your highlighted, underlined, or annotated content, go to
Contents
>
Annotations
. Here you can also export them to local storage, email, or other accounts to share with others.
To share the selected range of text in a reading card, you can also tap the
Share
button in the
Highlights & Underlines Menu
after selecting them.
To customize the export settings, do the following:
In NeoReader, tap the center area to display the main menu.
Tap the More icon in the top right corner and choose
Settings
>
Annotation Export Settings
.
Handwriting
Note:
Only available for models with handwriting functions.
To make Handwriting, do any of the following:
Tap the
Stylus
button in the main menu and turn off Use Stylus for Touch, and you can make handwriting with the stylus without the Handwriting Toolbar.
Tap the
Handwriting
button via the main menu or via the Floating Toolbar to access the Handwriting status with the Handwriting Toolbar.
Please refer to the
Floating Toolbar
page in the Reading chapter for more info about the Handwriting Toolbar.
To manage your handwriting, go to
Contents
>
Handwriting
. Here you can also export them in PNG or PDF format.
AI Smart Scribe
AI Smart Scribe enables you to handwrite notes in a smarter way. Tap the center area to bring up the
main menu
and tap the
AI icon
to discover all the new features.
Auto-Perfect Shapes
: Draw a perfect circle, rectangle, triangle, or straight line. Remember to finish the shape with
one stroke
and pause for
1 second
before lifting your stylus.
Mark Recognition
:
Highlight the marked content by underlining or circling sentences. You can choose the style and color of lines and highlights in the menu. The content you highlighted will be shown in
Contents＞Annotation
.
Tape keywords by drawing a wavy line on it to quiz yourself. And you can reveal the hidden areas by repeating the action.
Strikethrough Erase
: Draw a wavy line to erase unwanted notes or shapes. Remember to cover the unwanted content as much as possible for complete deletion. To avoid unintended erasing, with firmware
V3.5.1
, Strikethrough Erase will be disabled when you are using
Markers
.
Double-tap Recognition
: Double tap to convert your handwritten notes into text, or drawing into perfect shapes. You can manage your handwriting in
Contents＞Handwriting
.
Turn Pages with Stylus
: Use the stylus to tap the left or right side of the screen to turn pages.
Note
: Smart Scribe is only available in some models.
Notepads
Note:
Only available for models with handwriting functions.
Take Notes with Split View
To use notepads to take notes while reading books, do the following:
1. In NeoReader, tap the center area to display the main menu.
2. Choose
Split View
.
3. Choose your preferred Split View mode and tap
Handwritten
Note
or
Text
Note
. (The note will be created with the same name as the document and will be associated with it.)
or
1. In NeoReader, swipe down from the top right corner to open the
Control Center
.
2. Tap the
Split Screen
button.
3. Choose your preferred Split Screen mode.
4. Open a note in the Notes app on the other screen. (The note created will not be associated with the document.)
Please refer to the
Note-Taking
chapter for more info about the Notes app.
Take Notes by Adding Blank Pages
You can add blank pages in
PDF documents
for taking notes. The pages you added will be embedded in the document, which means you can export a PDF with your notes. You can delete unwanted blank pages in
Preview
. In addition, you can also
delete
added pages by tapping the icon next to the bookmark.
Reading Data Syncing
You can sync your reading data across all your BOOX devices, including reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, underlines, annotations, handwritten notes, and format for reflowable documents (optional). The annotated contents can be viewed and shared on our send2boox websites and BOOX Assistant app. Please refer to the
Reading Data Syncing
page for more info.
