Introducing Palma 2: Discover a Mindful Lifestyle with the New Potent Mobile ePaper
Oct 22, 2024
3 mins read
We are excited to introduce
Palma 2
, the new version of BOOX's most popular pocketable device. This
Android-based
mobile ePaper features a crisp
300 PPI
display, an
upgraded Octa-core CPU
, and a range of advanced software and hardware to let you reclaim your focus in the exact middle between tech and life.
Designed for a Clearer, Simpler Mindset
Palma 2
offers the perfect width for a comfortable one-handed grip and an ideal weight for extended use. Its sleek, phone-shaped design sets it apart from other ePaper devices. Available in two colors—Ivory White for a minimalist, elegant vibe and Deep Cyan Black for a more subtle, refined look—Palma 2 stands out with its exquisite craftsmanship. A gentle touch on the back reveals a carefully crafted rock texture that improves grip and adds a hint of natural beauty. Thoughtfully positioned on the sides, the
fingerprint power button
, smart button, and page-turn buttons provide a satisfying click with every press.
Tap open Palma 2, and you're greeted with a
minimalist
UI
. Clean lines, crisp icons, and the monochrome paper-like display create a fresh and elegant feel. Every aspect, from the layout to the buttons and input fields, is designed for both visual appeal and effortless usability. Whatever you're doing with Palma 2, it keeps your mind clear and helps you easily achieve a flow state.
Paperlike Serenity on Every Page
When you look at
Palma 2
, it feels as if your eyes are resting on real paper. With a
300 PPI Carta 1200 screen
, it delivers stunning contrast and clarity, mimicking the visual texture of premium paper. During reading, you'll find that every sentence is rendered crisp and serene. It's super pleasant to stare at for extended periods, ensuring
eye comfort
and sustained concentration.
Most Palma users' favorite is reading on
NeoReader
, BOOX's native reading app. Supporting up to 20 document formats, NeoReader allows you to tweak font size, boldness, spacing, and more to suit your preferences. With upgraded firmware, you can preset
reading themes
for novels, mangas, and research papers, or switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode, creating a truly personalized reading experience.
Palma 2 is equipped with dual-tone
front lights
and a light sensor, enabling you to dive into your favorite stories anytime. The gentle light sheds evenly on the paperlike screen, illuminating your chill moments day and night.
Power Up with Open Android
Powered by an upgraded Octa-core CPU,
Palma 2
delivers impressive performance for all your tasks. Paired with our exclusive
BOOX Super Refresh (BSR)
technology, you’ll experience fast app response, buttery scroll, and seamless task-switching. With four refresh modes to choose from, you can get the best balance between display quality and response for reading, browsing, and app use.
Based on
Android 13
, Palma 2 can be customized as your unique device. You can download apps from the pre-installed
Google Play Store
for time management, music, news, or habit tracking. The ample 6 GB+128 GB storage, together with a microSD card slot, offers sufficient space to store and run all your favorite apps. If you prefer a streamlined setup, the built-in apps—including BOOXDrop, NeoBrowser, Clock, and Calendar—provide everything you need for your daily routine without cluttering your device.
Versatility for Your Mindful Lifestyle
Built to be a companion of your mindful living,
Palma 2
is more versatile than you might expect. Beyond its excellent reading features, Palma 2's advanced hardware and software enable it to play multiple roles in your life. Dual microphones and one speaker turn it into a
portable audio player
for music and podcasts, while the 16MP camera with LED flash functions as a handy
document scanner
in the office. The latest firmware includes an
AI
assistant
, ready to answer your questions whenever they crop up.
Thanks to the flexibility of the open Android system, you can easily download productivity apps to schedule your to-dos and record ideas, making life more organized and sparking creativity. Compact and portable, Palma 2 can accompany you on commutes, walks, or trips. The inherent long battery life of E Ink devices allows Palma 2 to sip power instead of gulping it, ensuring lasting companionship.
Wrap Up
With its paper-like display, robust performance, and the flexibility of Android, Palma 2 has everything you'd expect from a portable ePaper device. Now it's available on
BOOX Shop
, take yours and embrace a distraction-free life.
