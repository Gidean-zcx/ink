USING DUOLINGO
What is a streak?
Your streak (flame icon) represents the number of days in a row you’ve completed a lesson on Duolingo.
Language learning is about building goals over time, and the streak is a proven way to motivate you to keep learning and practicing every day.
Tip: Practice reminders can be a great help for remembering to do your lessons. In your notification settings you can turn on practice reminders and set the time that will work best for you.
What is a streak freeze?
Sometimes you need a break! A streak freeze lets you maintain your streak when you forget to practice for a day.
A streak freeze must be purchased in advance of the day of a missed lesson to protect the streak.
But if you forget to purchase a streak freeze and you lose your streak, no worries! You can also purchase a streak repair with gems (available in the Shop). Head over to the Shop tab to purchase your streak freeze.
On the Duolingo app (iOS or Android):
Open the Duolingo app
Sign in to your Duolingo account
Tap the gem icon at the top of the screen to go to the Shop
Tap “Streak Freeze” and confirm
On your web browser (desktop):
Sign in to your Duolingo account via web browser
Click “Shop” in the side navigation
Tap “Streak Freeze” and confirm
How do I get gems in Duolingo?
Gems are Duolingo’s virtual in-app currency. (In the web version, they’re called lingots.)
You’ll earn gems as you complete lessons and quests. If you don’t have enough gems to purchase an item in the Shop, you can purchase packs of gems in the Shop.
