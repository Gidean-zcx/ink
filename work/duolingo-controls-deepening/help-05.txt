How does matchmaking work in Duolingo Chess?
When you opt into live matchmaking in Duolingo Chess, we try to find a balanced opponent as quickly as possible. We start by looking for another Duolingo learner within ±50 of your rating and gradually expand this window as needed to avoid long waiting times.
In certain cases, for example if you’re new to Duolingo Chess, we may strictly pair you with learners with a lower rating (within -50) or with a bot. This helps new learners build confidence before facing tougher opponents. However, learners with a rating of 700 or higher are not paired with bots.
