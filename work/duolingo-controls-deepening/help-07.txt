How do I find, follow, and block users on Duolingo?
You can connect with other learners on Duolingo!
When you follow someone, they’ll show up on your friends list and you can encourage each other to stick with your language goals!
Where do I view my friends on Duolingo?
On the Duolingo app:
Go to your Profile
Tap “Following” to view your friends list
On the web version:
Go to your Profile
Scroll down to view your friends list
Your Friends list is ordered by their all-time XP. If you want to compare stats, you can tap or click on their name to compare weekly XP and view their streak and league stats and achievements.
How do I follow someone on Duolingo?
When you land on their profile page, click the “Follow” button.
They will be notified that they have a new follower. They can also follow you back. If they block you, you can no longer follow them. The maximum number of followers is currently 1,000.
How do I unfollow someone on Duolingo?
Tap on the “Following” button and that will unfollow the user.
How do I block or report someone on Duolingo?
Blocking someone means they won’t be able to follow you on Duolingo.
To block a user on the Duolingo app:
Go to your Profile
Tap “Following” to view your friends list
Tap on the user’s name
Scroll down and tap “Block user” then confirm
To block a user on the web version:
Go to your Profile
Click on the user’s name via your friends list
Hover over the three dots menu and tap “Block”
If you change your mind later, you can unblock a user by tapping “Unblock user.”
How do I report a user?
Reporting someone means you’re alerting Duolingo that the user may have violated community guidelines, which could mean their account will be removed.
To report a user, go to their profile and then tap or click “Report.” Or, you can “submit a bug report” and select “Report abuse” as the type of issue. If possible please include a screenshot and the usernames of those involved so that we can investigate.
If I can’t follow others, what can I do?
Check that your email is verified
If your account is inactive, complete a lesson then try again
If you set your profile to private or are under 13 years of age, you cannot follow others or be followed
How can I congratulate my friends?
Once you follow a friend on Duolingo, you can encourage and motivate each other as you reach key learning milestones. The app will notify you when your friend has a milestone to celebrate, and when your friend next opens Duolingo they’ll see your congratulatory message!
You can also share your personal milestones by tapping the “Share” button and selecting a social platform.
How do I make my profile private?
Go to your privacy settings here: https://www.duolingo.com/settings/privacy
Uncheck “Make my profile public”
Note: If you make your profile private, it will disable leaderboards and friends.
