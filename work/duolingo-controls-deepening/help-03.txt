USING DUOLINGO
What are leaderboards and leagues?
What are leaderboards?
Leaderboards are a fun way to compete with other Duolingo learners in a weekly contest. As you earn more XP (experience points) with each lesson, you’ll rise in the ranks of your leaderboard. You’ll face a new group of competitors each week. Check out the Leaderboards tab in the app to get the competition started!
What are leagues?
The top XP earners in your leaderboard will be promoted to a higher league for the following week. The lower XP earners will be demoted to the previous league.
Leagues, from lowest to highest: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Sapphire, Ruby, Emerald, Amethyst, Pearl, Obsidian, and Diamond.
When do leaderboards end and restart?
Leaderboards end and restart Sunday afternoon or evening, based on your device’s timezone. The leaderboard tab includes a countdown showing when it will end.
If I don’t see leaderboards, what can I do?
You can check whether your profile is private. If you would like to have access to leaderboards:
Go to your privacy settings here: https://www.duolingo.com/settings/privacy
Check “Make my profile public”
If you are under 13 years old you won’t have access to social features including leaderboards. If you are in a classroom, be sure to check your classroom settings.
How do I disable leaderboard-related notifications?
In your “notification settings” you can turn on or off notifications about your leaderboard standings.
How do I disable leagues/leaderboards?
If you make your profile private, it will disable leaderboards and friends. To make your profile private:
Go to your privacy settings here: https://www.duolingo.com/settings/privacy
Uncheck “Make my profile public”
