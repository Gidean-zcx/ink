L17: # Capture your ideas & notes with Gemini Apps
L18: 
L19: You can ask Gemini Apps to create and show your notes and lists in Google Keep, HONOR Notes, Lenovo Notes, OnePlus Notes, OPPO Notes, Samsung Notes, TECNO Notepad, vivo Notes, and Xiaomi Notes. You can also ask them to add items to existing lists in Google Keep.
L20: ## What you need
L21: 
L22: To connect your notes and lists apps to Gemini Apps, you need to:
L23: 
L24:   * Be signed in to the Gemini app. 【2†Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps】.
L25: 
L26:   * Have 【3†Keep Activity†myactivity.google.com】 on. Gemini can’t connect to these apps when this setting is off.
L27: 
L28: Important:
L29:   * The supported notes and lists apps aren't available in Gemini in Google Messages.
L30:   * Gemini Apps can hallucinate responses or provide outdated information. To check the information, click and review the sources listed after the response.
L31: ## Connect your notes & lists to Gemini Apps
L32: 
L33: Connect Google Keep
L34: 
L35: To work with your notes and lists from Google Keep in Gemini Apps, Google Workspace must be connected to Gemini Apps.
L36:   1. On your phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app 【4†Image†lh3.googleusercontent.com】 or go to 【5†gemini.google.com†gemini.google.com】.
L37:   2. Make sure you’re signed in to the same account you use with Google Workspace.
L38:   3. Ask the Gemini app to create or find a list from Google Keep.
L39:      * Tip: To tell Gemini Apps to specifically use Google Keep, add `@Google Keep` to your prompt.
L40:   4. If Google Workspace isn’t connected, you’ll get the option to connect it.
L41:   5. Follow the on-screen instructions.

L109: Tips:
L110:   * You can disconnect these apps anytime in your Connected Apps settings. 【6†Learn how to manage Connected Apps in Gemini】.
L111:   * If you connect multiple notes and lists apps, the Gemini app defaults to the last one it accessed. To manage a different app, you can specify it in your prompt by adding `@` and selecting the app.
L112:   * You can also manage your calendar events for some apps in your Gemini Live chats. 【7†Learn how to use Connected Apps in Live chats】.
L113:   * You can also say 'Hey Google' to ask Gemini to use a connected app. If "Hey Google" doesn’t work, 【8†check if “Hey Google” and Voice Match are set up】.
L114: ### What Gemini Apps can’t do
L115: 
L116: Gemini Apps can’t perform certain actions in your notes and lists apps for you, like:
L117: 
L118:   * Share notes and lists
L119:   * View images and drawings
L120:   * Add an image
L121: 
L122: In addition, Gemini Apps can’t currently modify a note or delete notes and lists in Samsung Notes or Xiaomi notes. Gemini Apps also can’t delete a shared note owned by someone else.
L123: ## Create a note or list
L124: 
L125: Create notes & lists by giving specific details
L126: 
L127:   * Create a note called [note name]
L128:   * Create a list called [list name] with [item 1, item 2, …]
L129: 
L130: Create notes & lists based on your conversation
L131: 
L132: You can ask Gemini Apps to perform a task and create a note or list. For example:
L133:   * Recommend some activities for [event] and save this to Keep.
L134:   * Make an outline for [topic].
L135: 【9†Image: After Gemini's response:†lh3.googleusercontent.com】 @Google Keep save this.
L136:   * Tell me what steps I need to take to [goal or task]. Then create a list in Keep.
L137:   * Find a recipe for [dish] and create a list of the ingredients in Keep.
L138: 
L139: Tips:
L140:   * To create a note in Google Keep, you can add “Save this to Keep” or “@Google Keep save this.” Or, to create a list in Google Keep, add “Create a list in Keep” or “Create a list of the [items, steps, ingredients, …] in Keep.”
L141:   * To create a note in another app, you can add “Save this to @[app name]” or “@[app name] save this.”
L142: ## Add item to an existing list in Google Keep
L143: 
L144: Add item to an existing list
L145: 
L146:   * Add [item 1, item 2, …] to my list called [list name]
L147:   * Include [item 1, item 2, …] to [list name] list
L148: 
L149: Tip: On supported Samsung mobile devices, the Gemini mobile app can add an item to an existing list in the Samsung Reminder app if the app is connected in your Connected Apps settings.
L150: 
L151: Add item to an existing list based on your conversation
L152: You can ask Gemini Apps to perform a task and add an item to an existing list in Google Keep. For example:
L153: 
L154:   * What materials will I need for [activity or task]?
L155: 【9†Image: After Gemini's response:†lh3.googleusercontent.com】 Add this to my [list name] list.
L156:   * Find me a recipe for [dish] and save the ingredients to Keep.
L157: Tip: To undo an action, select Undo in the confirmation notification, or open and edit the list linked below the response. If you created a note with Gemini Live on in the background, Gemini can’t undo the note.
L158: ## Get content from your notes & lists
L159: 
L160: Find content from your notes & lists
L161: 
L162:   * Do I have any notes related to [topic]?
L163:   * Find my [list name] list
L164:   * Show all my lists about [task, event, goal, …]
L165: 
L166: Use your notes & lists in your conversation
L167: 
L168: You can use your notes or lists to ask Gemini Apps to perform a task. For example:
L169: 
L170:   * Help me create a [song, story, …] based on my Keep note called [note name]
L171:   * Suggest a [dish, activity, …] from the items in my [list name] list
