# How can I download the weekly edition?

How can I download the weekly edition on the app?

There are two options available to download the weekly edition onto your device—make sure you are using the most up to date version of the app (v4.30+) to access these features. 

 

You can do this automatically from the Download new editions toggle in the Settings menu. This will happen overnight on a Thursday, and download all content from the most recent edition. This will include audio and text to allow you to read and listen. You must have background app refresh switched on to allow the app to download overnight.

 

If you do not wish to enable the automatic download, you can manually download each edition of your choice by going to the Weekly tab and tapping Download edition. For other week’s, go to the Weekly tab and tap Browse editions in the top right corner. This will show all editions in reverse date order. Click on your edition of choice and then Download edition to start the download. You can simultaneously download multiple editions. 

 
How many downloaded editions can I keep on my device?
The app will store up to eight editions on your device. If more than eight editions are downloaded, the edition that was downloaded first will be overwritten.
To view the downloaded editions, go to the Weekly tab and select Browse editions in the top right corner. This will show all editions in reverse date order. A downloaded edition can be identified by the presence of a download icon (down arrow). To delete an edition, click into the edition and tap Delete. 
We are currently working on adding an option to download text and images without audio.
