To start a screen recording, do the following:
  1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the Control Center.
  2. Find and tap the Screen Recorder button in the Shortcut Switches.
  3. Tap the ⚪ button to start recording. Then tap the ❌ button to end the recording. The recordings will be saved in Storage/Movies/Captures in mp4 format.
  4. You can tap the thumbnail to view it directly or tap the Share button to share it with others.
Note: If you cannot find the Screen Recorder button on the Control Center, you may do the following:
  1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the Control Center.
  2. Tap the Edit icon.
  3. Drag and drop the Screen Recorder button to the enabled Shortcut Switches.
 
To customize the screen-recording settings before starting, do the following:
  1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the Control Center.
  2. Long press the Screen Recorder button in the Shortcut Switches.
  3. Here you can customize settings including resolution, quality, framerate and more.
