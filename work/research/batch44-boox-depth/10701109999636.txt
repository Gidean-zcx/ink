The BOOX Tab Series devices are equipped with a speaker for audio output. In this article, you will learn how to adjust the volume of your device.
1. Swipe down from the top right corner to open the Control Center and drag the volume slider.
2. Long press the volume icon to adjust the volume of specific functions
3. Tap the Mute button in the Control Center to silence the Ring & Notification Volume
4. Adjust the volume by setting Side Gestures: Settings＞System Navigation＞Side Gestures
