You can do the following to take a screenshot.
Method 1: Via the Control Center
Swipe down from the top right corner to open the Control Center.
Find and tap the Screenshot button in the Shortcut Switches.
You can also use the Scrolling Screenshot to capture long web pages.
Note:
If you cannot find the Screenshot button on the Control Center, you may do the following: open the Control Center>tap the Edit icon>Drag and drop the Screenshot or Scrolling Screenshot button to add it.
Due to privacy and copyright protections, some apps and websites may limit screenshots.
Method 2: Via the NaviBall
1. Tap the Navigation Ball.
2. Tap the Screenshot button.
Note:
1. The Screenshot button is included in the NaviBall menu by default. Settings on your device may vary. Please refer to the NaviBall page for more info.
2. You can add the Scrolling Screenshot to your NaviBall by tapping the Setting icon on the menu.
Method 3: Via Gestures
1. Swipe down the screen with three fingers to take a quick screenshot.
2. You can customize gestures in Settings > System Navigation > Gestures.
