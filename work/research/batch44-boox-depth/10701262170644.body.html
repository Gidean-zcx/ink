In this article, you will learn more about the app management settings on your device.
 
To access the app management settings, go to Settings > Apps & Notifications in Home Screen.
 
 
Application
Here you can manage or uninstall your apps.
 
Permission
Here you can manage the permission settings of your apps.
 
Notifications
Here you can manage the notification settings of your apps.
 
Default app
Here you can change the default app when opening a new file.
 
App Startup
Here you can enable / disable the auto start settings of your apps.
 
Note: The auto start functions will increase power consumption and may cause the device to run slow.
 
App Refresh Mode
Here you can manage the refresh mode settings for each app.
 
DPI Setting
Here you can manage the DPI settings for each app.
 
Force to use system orientation
Here you can manage the settings to force each app to follow the system direction.
 
Scrolling Button Setting
Here you can manage the scrolling button settings for each app.
 
Work Profile
Here you can enable a work profile to help you keep your personal and work data separate. Please refer to the Work Profile page for more info.
 
Note: This function is only available for select models.
 
Store
Here you can enable / disable the built-in Store.
 
Freeze Settings
Here you can manage the freeze settings for some apps to optimize the performance of the device and save power.
 
Note: Using the Freeze functions will prevent the app from any operation in the background such as sending notifications.
 
Reset App Optimization Tutorial
Here you can go through the App Optimization Tutorial again when opening a third-party app.
 
Reset all app optimization
Here you can reset all app optimization settings to the original ones for all apps.
