Run shortcuts on iPad with Apple Pencil Pro
If you’re using an iPad with an Apple Pencil Pro, you can quickly squeeze Apple Pencil Pro to trigger a shortcut.
Configure Apple Pencil Pro to run a shortcut
On an iPad with an Apple Pencil Pro, go to Settings > Apple Pencil > Squeeze.
Tap Shortcut, then select a shortcut.See alsoIntro to how shortcuts work on iPhone and iPad
