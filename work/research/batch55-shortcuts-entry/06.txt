Use Control Center to run shortcuts
You can store your most used shortcuts in Control Center for easy and convenient access.
Add a shortcut to Control Center
Open Control Center by swiping down from the top right, or swiping up from the bottom (depending on your device).
Tap at the top left, then tap Add a Control at the bottom.
Tap Shortcut, tap Choose, then select which shortcut to run.See alsoIntro to variables in Shortcuts on iPhone or iPad
