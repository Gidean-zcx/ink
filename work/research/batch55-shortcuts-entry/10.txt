Run shortcuts from the Search screen on your iPhone or iPad
Using search, you can quickly locate and open a shortcut without having to open the Shortcuts app.
On the Home Screen of your iPhone or iPad, swipe down from the center.
Type the name of a shortcut in the search field.
A list of results forms as you type. You may need to scroll to locate your shortcuts. Each shortcut result displays the icon and name of the shortcut.
Tap the shortcut, then do one of the following:
Tap “Run,” then follow any additional onscreen instructions.
Tap the shortcut to open the Shortcuts app, then tap to run the shortcut.
