Intro to editing shortcuts on iPhone or iPad
As you build your shortcut collection, there are a number of ways you can organize and edit shortcuts. You can:
Reorder shortcuts so that the ones you use most appear first in the Shortcuts app, in the Shortcuts widget on iOS and iPadOS, and on Apple Watch
Edit a shortcut by describing a change in plain language
Quickly duplicate a shortcut for use as the starting point of a new shortcut
Delete a shortcut you no longer need
Share shortcuts, and customize a shared shortcut’s information so that no personal data is shared
Sync shortcuts between devices
Create folders to organize shortcuts into custom categories
