Meet Sea Turtles
There are seven species of sea turtles. Each species has a shell for protection. Each species has strong flippers for swimming. Learn more about sea turtles.
Turtle Diet
This sea turtle is chasing a jellyfish. Some sea turtles also eat plants. They eat seaweed and algae.
A Long Journey
Sea turtles live in the water. But they lay eggs on land. Baby turtles hurry to the ocean when they hatch.
Sharp Eyes
Sea turtles have good eyesight. They can see in dark water. And they can see colors. Their eyesight helps them hunt.
Turtles in Trouble
There is trash in the ocean. That is a problem for turtles. See this bag? A turtle might think it is food.

Saving Sea Turtles
Grace Buschiazzo is a marine biologist. She works at the South Carolina Aquarium. She is part of its Sea Turtle Care Center.
Turtle Rescue
Sea turtles can get sick. They can get hurt. The team at the care center treats them.
A Trip to the Doctor
This turtle is getting scanned. It has to be strong and healthy. Then it can go back to the sea.
Good as New
Another turtle swallowed fishing line. Doctors did surgery. They removed it. The turtle was saved.
