This section explains the way Anki is installed, and the possible problems you may encounter, depending on your OS:

* [macOS](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/platform/mac/intro.md)
* [Linux](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/platform/linux/intro.md)
