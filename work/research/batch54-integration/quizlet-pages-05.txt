L13: # Navigating your home feed on mobile devices
L14: 
L15: The Quizlet app now has a smarter home feed to help you study more effectively. Your feed is personalized with quick study activities, progress updates, and recommendations based on what you’re learning.
L16: ## What’s in your home feed
L17: 
L18: The personalized home feed is available on the Quizlet app for iOS and Android. Once onboarding is complete, students will see a tailored feed featuring:
L19: 
L20:   * Study Bites - quick, bite-sized study activities like 【9†Flashcards】 or 【10†Match】
L21:   * Progress Cards -  jump back into your recent study activity
L22:   * Content Recommendations - suggested study content based on your courses and activity
L23: Personalized home is currently optimized for U.S. college students but is available globally for learners ages 13+.
L24: ## FAQs
L25: 
L26: How do I access the home feed?
L27: 
L28: Open the Quizlet app on your mobile device. After answering some questions, you’ll be taken directly to your personalized feed.
L29: 
L30: Can I change my courses?
L31: 
L32: Yes! You can 【11†update your school information】 at any time in your settings to improve the relevance of your recommendations.
L33: 
L34: I don’t see any Study Bites. What should I do?
L35: 
L36: Study Bites appear once you begin studying. Try studying a 【12†flashcard set or using Learn】 to activate them.
L37: Where is all of my other study content?
L38: 
L39: You can find all of your study content in your Library.
