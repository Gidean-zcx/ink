Title: Playing a game of Blocks
Platforms shown: iOS, Web, Android

Rule and flow extracted:
- A set needs at least six terms and definitions.
- Web: log in -> set -> Blocks -> Play. App text starts from opening a set, then Blocks -> Play.
- Drag three pieces onto the board; complete rows/columns to earn points.
- After placing all three pieces, answer a question correctly to receive three more pieces.
- A question can be refreshed to try another.
- The game ends when the player runs out of moves; the page frames the result against a top score.
- Options can restrict study to starred terms and choose the question format.
- In certain locations, younger users need parental consent confirmed by email before play.

Not stated in the extracted official text: manual quit/cancel behavior; whether refresh has a penalty; wrong-answer feedback; top-score storage/sync/reset; subscription boundary.
