Title: Playing a game of Blast
Platforms shown: iOS, Web, Android

Rule and flow extracted:
- Players individually match terms and definitions shown on moving asteroids; the first to hit the correct asteroid scores, and the highest score wins when time expires.
- Host eligibility: account identified as teacher; English set with at least six terms and definitions.
- Host: log in on website -> set -> Blast -> Get Started -> choose prompt/answer combination -> project host screen -> players join at quizlet.live by code/name or app QR -> Create game -> Start game.
- Players are assigned to groups; the article suggests seating group members together where possible.
- Options change asteroid speed, number of choices, and game duration.
- Hosting is website-only; players may join/play on mobile devices.
- Unavailable in Canada and the United Kingdom; unavailable for diagram sets.

Not stated in the extracted official text: host cancel/early-end controls; disconnect/rejoin; late join; tie handling; retained scores/history; paid subscription boundary.
