L13: # Studying with Audio Notes
L14: 
L15: 【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】
L16: 
L17: Record or upload audio from a lecture to generate a Study Guide and flashcard set. Quizlet transcribes and summarizes your recording, helping you turn lectures and other course materials into study resources.
L18: 
L19: On the Quizlet website, you can upload an existing audio file to create a Study Guide.
L20: 
L21: To create a Study Guide by uploading audio
L22:   1. 【9†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
L23:   2. Select Create.
L24:   3. Select Study Guide.
L25:   4. Select Select file.
L26:   5. Select an audio file from your device.
L27: 
L28: Supported file types are `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, and `.aac`. Audio recordings can be up to 5 hours long and files can be up to 500 MB.
L29: 
L30:   6. Enter a title and select a folder, if you'd like.
L31:   7. Select the language used in the audio.
L32:   8. Select Generate.
L33: Quizlet uploads and processes your audio, then creates a Study Guide and a linked flashcard set. You can leave the Study Guide while Quizlet finishes processing it.
L34: 
L35: Recording audio isn't supported on the mobile website. To record audio, use the Quizlet app for iOS or Android.
L36: 
L37: To continue an interrupted upload
L38: 
L39: If you leave before your recording finishes uploading, Quizlet saves it as a draft on your device.
L40:   1. Open Your library.
L41:   2. Select Study Guides.
L42:   3. Find the draft under In progress.
L43:   4. Select the draft.
L44:   5. Open the options menu and select Continue uploading.
L45: 
L46: To fix a Study Guide generated in the wrong language
L47: 
L48: The selected audio language determines the language Quizlet uses to process the recording. This can happen if the wrong audio language was selected before the audio was processed.
L49:   1. Open the Study Guide.
L50:   2. Select More options.
L51:   3. Select View original upload.
L52:   4. Download the original audio file.
L53:   5. Create a new Study Guide.
L54:   6. Upload the audio file again.
L55:   7. Select the correct audio language before generating the Study Guide.
L56: 
L57: Settings and options
L58:   * Study Guide summaries created from audio are available in English, French, and German.
L59:   * Practice Tests can't be generated from audio.
L60:   * The generated Study Guide and flashcard set are saved to your library.
L61:   * Audio Notes is available in eligible countries to users who meet Quizlet’s age requirements.
L62:   * Recording audio is available in the Quizlet iOS and Android apps. You can upload supported audio files on the web.
L63:   * Free and Quizlet Plus subscribers can process a limited number of audio recordings. 【10†Quizlet Plus Unlimited†quizlet.com】 subscribers have unlimited access to recordings and uploads.
L64: AI-generated content may contain errors. Review your Study Guide and flashcards before studying.
L65: 
L66: *If you’re under the required age, you are unable to record your own audio until you reach your region’s age of consent. Find out more about 【11†younger users’ account limitations】.

L13: # Studying with Ask Quizlet
L14: 
L15: Ask Quizlet is a new way to study directly within Quizlet. Instead of jumping between tools, Ask Quizlet helps learners get clear, step-by-step explanations grounded in what they’re studying, turn those explanations into study materials like flashcards, and find relevant study sets so they know what to focus on next.
L16: 
L17: Ask Quizlet supports several study flows, depending on what you ask:
L18:   * Explain a key concept to help build understanding.
L19:   * Find study materials related to a topic.
L20:   * See if a set is right for you when accessed from a set page.
L21:   * Instantly generate flashcards that you can edit and save.
L22:   * Upload files or images to ask questions about your study materials
L23: 
L24: If you ask a question related to account or product support, Ask Quizlet will direct you to the Quizlet Help Center.
L25: 
L26: To study with Ask Quizlet
L27:   1. 【9†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
L28:   2. Open Ask Quizlet* from the Home page or from a set page.
L29:   3. Type what you want to study. You can also upload a file or image and include a prompt about what you need help with.
L30:   4. Review the response provided.
L31:   5. Use the suggested actions to continue studying, such as opening a study set, generating flashcards, or starting a study session.
L32: 
L33: Tips
L34:   * Be specific about what you want to study to get the most relevant results.
L35:   * Ask follow-up questions to continue learning or clarify a concept.
L36:   * Upload notes, screenshots, or supported files when you want Ask Quizlet to respond based on your study materials.
L37:   * You can upload up to 3 files at a time. Supported file types include .pptx, .docx, .pdf, .png, .jpg, and .jpeg.
L38:   * Uploaded files and images can be used to ask questions, get explanations, summarize content, generate quizzes, or create flashcards.
L39:   * On mobile, uploaded files can’t exceed 25 MB.
L40:   * Generated flashcard sets are fully editable, just like any other Quizlet set.
L41:   * Chat history isn’t saved across pages. If you leave Ask Quizlet, the conversation will reset, but minimizing it will keep your chat active.
L42: *Ask Quizlet is currently only available for users in the United States who are 14 years old or older.
