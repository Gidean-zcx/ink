How To Find Your Phone Number on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:35 AM
You can find the phone numbers for each of your installed SIM cards in the SIM status screen.
You can find the phone numbers for each of your installed SIM cards in the SIM status screen.
 
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap About
Tap SIM status (sim slot #) for the SIM you want to view
