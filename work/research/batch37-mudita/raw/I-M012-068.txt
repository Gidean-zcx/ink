How To Manage Mobile Data and Roaming on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:41 AM
How To Allow or Stop Downloads Using Data
If you stop Kompakt from using mobile data, downloads will need a Wi-Fi connection.
This is useful if you have a limited data allowance or high cost for data.
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Network and Internet
Tap Mobile Network
Tap the Mobile data Toggle switch to stop or allow Kompakt using mobile data
How to allow or stop roaming
Roaming is when you use your device outside of your home country.
Your network provider can charge you extra for this feature.
Always check with your network provider before allowing roaming!
To allow roaming:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Network and Internet
Tap Mobile Network
Tap the Roaming Toggle switch to stop or allow roaming
