How To Create a Calendar Event on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:40 AM
To create a calendar event:
Tap today's date on the home screen*
Tap the left and right arrows to select the month
Tap the Day you want your event on
Tap the Plus icon
Tap Add Title to customize the even name
Tap the up and down arrows to select the start time
Tap the up and down arrows to select the end time
Tap Create
*You can also unhide the app icon to enable access by tapping Apps > Calendar
