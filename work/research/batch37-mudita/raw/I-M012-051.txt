How to Transfer Photos to Kompakt?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
You can easily transfer photos to your Kompakt using Mudita Center. Follow the steps below to upload your images 
Open Mudita Center and connect your Kompakt to your computer.
Under Your Kompakt, click Manage Files.
Select the Photos tab.
Click Add file.
Browse your computer and select the image files you want to upload.
Confirm the transfer to copy the selected photos to your Kompakt.
