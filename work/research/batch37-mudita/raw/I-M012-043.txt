How to Create a CSV File Using Outlook?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:34 AM
You can export your contacts from Outlook as a CSV file to use them later in Mudita Center
Exporting Contacts from Outlook
On your computer, sign in to your Outlook account using your Microsoft ID.
Click the People icon.
Click Manage contacts.
Click Export.
Click Export again to confirm.
The CSV file will be saved in your Downloads folder.
