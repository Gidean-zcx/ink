How To Manage Alarms on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:45 AM
How to create an alarm
To create your first alarm: 
Tap the clock on the home screen*
Tap Create an Alarm
You can edit name and sound and the days it repeats
Tap Create
To add additional alarms: 
Tap the clock on the home screen*
Tap the Plus icon 
You can edit name and sound and the days it repeats
Tap Create 
How to edit an alarm 
Tap the clock on the home screen*
Tap on the time Alarm you want to edit 
Edit the time using the up and down arrows or using the keypad
You can edit name and sound and the days it repeats
Tap Save
How to delete an alarm 
Tap the clock on the home screen*
Tap the Pencil icon
Tap the Delete icon 
How to snooze an alarm
Snooze is fixed to a 5 minute interval and is not automatic.
To snooze the alarm:
Wait until your alarm rings
Tap Snooze
*You can also unhide the app icon to enable access by tapping Apps > Alarm
