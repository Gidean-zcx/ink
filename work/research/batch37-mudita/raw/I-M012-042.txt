How to create a CSV or vCard using Google Account?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:38 AM
You can export your contacts from your Google Account as a CSV or vCard (VCF file) to use them later in Mudita Center
Exporting Contacts from Google
On your computer, sign in to your Google account using your Google ID.
Click the Grid icon to open the list of Google apps.
Select Contacts.
Choose the contacts you want to export.
Click the three dots (More actions) icon.
Click Export.
Select the format you want to export:
CSV
vCard (VCF)
Click Export again to confirm.
The CSV or vCard file will be saved in your Downloads folder.
To quickly select all contacts:
Click the down arrow icon.
Select All.
