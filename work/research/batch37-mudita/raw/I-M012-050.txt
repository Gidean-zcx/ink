How To Transfer Photos from Mudita Kompakt to Your Computer
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:36 AM
You can currently transfer photos using Windows File Manager or by sending MMS messages.
To transfer photos using Windows:
Connect Kompakt to a computer with a USB-C cable*
Access Kompakt’s files
Open the DCIM folder**
Select the photos you want to transfer and copy them to your computer
* If files don't show when connecting Kompakt you need to allow file transfer
** For screenshots open the Pictures folder
