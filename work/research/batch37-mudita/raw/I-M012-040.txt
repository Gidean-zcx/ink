How to import contacts from CSV file or vCard?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:40 AM
You can import contacts to your Kompakt device using a CSV file or a vCard (VCF) file through Mudita Center
Before importing your contacts, you need to prepare a supported contact file:
Create a CSV file by exporting contacts from Outlook.
Create a vCard (VCF) file from your Google account.
Create a vCard (VCF) file from iCloud.
Make sure that:
Mudita Center is installed on your computer.
Your Kompakt device is connected to your computer.
The CSV or VCF file is saved on your computer and ready to import.
Importing Contacts from a CSV File or vCard
Open Mudita Center and connect your Kompakt device.
In the left-hand menu, navigate to Your Kompakt and select Contacts.
Click Import contacts.
Select Upload CSV or vCard.
Choose the CSV or VCF file from your computer.
Select the contacts you want to import, or choose All contacts to import the entire list.
Click Import selected contacts to start transferring the contacts to your phone.
Once the process is complete, the selected contacts will be available on your Kompakt device.
