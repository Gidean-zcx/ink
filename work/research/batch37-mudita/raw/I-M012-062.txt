How to Take a Screenshot?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:34 AM
Taking a screenshot on your Kompakt is a quick way to capture what’s currently displayed on your screen
To take a screenshot, press and hold the Power button and the Volume Down button at the same time.
Once the buttons are pressed together, the screenshot will be captured and automatically saved to your device.
