Does Mudita Kompakt Support Video Recording and Playback?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:32 AM
The Mudita Kompakt does not support video recording or playback. We might consider adding video support in the future, but for now, the Kompakt can only capture and display photos.
