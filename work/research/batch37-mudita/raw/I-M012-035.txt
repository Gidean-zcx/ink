How to edit an alarm?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:36 AM
You can easily modify existing alarms on your Mudita Kompakt
Edit an alarm
Tap the clock on the Home screen.
Select the alarm time you want to edit.
Adjust the time using the up and down arrows or the keypad.
(Optional) Update the alarm name, sound, and repeat schedule.
Tap Save to confirm your changes.
Access the Alarm app
If the Alarm app icon is hidden, you can access it by navigating to: Apps → Alarm
From there, you can manage all your alarms, including editing, enabling, disabling, or deleting them.
