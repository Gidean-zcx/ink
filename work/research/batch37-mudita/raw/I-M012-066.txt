How To Use Wi-Fi and Create a Hotspot on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:45 AM
How to connect to Wi-Fi
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Network and Internet
Tap Wi-Fi
Select the Wi-Fi network you want to join 
Enter the Wi-Fi network password
Tap Connect 
How to share Wi-Fi password
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Network and Internet
Tap Wi-Fi
Select the Wi-Fi network you are connected to 
Tap Share
Touch the power button to scan your fingerprint or tap USE PIN > Enter PIN
Either scan the QR code with the other device, or copy the Wi-Fi passcode manually
How to create a hotspot
Hotspots allow other devices to use Kompakt’s Wi-Fi or data when they are tethered.
To create a hotspot:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Network and Internet
Tap Hotspot and Tethering
Tap Wi-Fi Hotspot
You can edit the name, password* and security settings for the hotspot
To turn on the hotspot Tap the Toggle Switch near the top of the screen 
*The password must be 8 or more characters long
You can also turn the hotspot on and off from the Control Center: 
Press the Control Center button
Tap Hotspot icon which is above the Airplane icon
