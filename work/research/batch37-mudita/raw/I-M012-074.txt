Does Mudita Kompakt Have a Messaging App Like WhatsApp or Signal?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
The initial release of Mudita Kompakt does not include a dedicated messaging app like WhatsApp or Signal. The Kompakt does have a built-in app for core SMS and MMS messaging support, with free software updates planned for future enhancements.
While we’re exploring options for adding messaging functionalities in future updates, this will depend on technical feasibility and user feedback.
Third-Party Messaging Apps
There is no app store on the Mudita Kompakt to install third-party apps. If you’re considering sideloading them, please refer to the following article to learn about important details regarding compatibility with MuditaOS K and E-ink screen performance:
