Can the Mudita Kompakt Take Screenshots?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:33 AM
Yes! You can take a screenshot by pressing the power button and volume down button simultaneously. The phone won't show any indication of taking the screenshot, but you'll be able to find the screen view that you captured in the gallery.
