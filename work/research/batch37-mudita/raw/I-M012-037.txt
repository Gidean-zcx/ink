How to snooze an alarm?
Modified on: Thu, 17 Sep, 2026 at 1:21 PM
The snooze function on Mudita Kompakt uses a fixed 5-minute interval and is not automatic
Snooze an alarm
Wait until the alarm starts ringing.
Tap Snooze on the screen.
The alarm will ring again after 5 minutes.
