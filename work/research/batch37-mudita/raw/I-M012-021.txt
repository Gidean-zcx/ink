How to Switch Between SIM 2 and eSIM Mode?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:34 AM
To switch between SIM 2 and eSIM mode:
Open Control Center and tap Settings.
Go to Network & Internet > SIM Cards.
Tap Switch mode.
Tap Switch mode again to confirm.
Kompakt will restart automatically to apply the change.
Note: If you haven't set up an eSIM yet, install it before switching modes.
