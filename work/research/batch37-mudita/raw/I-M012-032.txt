How To Download and Delete Maps on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:42 AM
How to download a map
To view a region or country on the maps app, you will need to download the map.
This requires an internet connection. Once you have downloaded the map, you won’t need the internet to search or view the map.    
To download a map:
Tap Apps
Tap Maps > Tap Continue*
Tap the Menu icon
Tap Manage Maps
Tap the Search icon** 
Enter the country or region name of the map you want
Tap the Download icon
*Only when using maps for the first time
**Or tap All Maps and select the region you want
How to delete a map
Tap Apps
Tap Maps
Tap the Menu icon
Tap Manage Maps
Tap the Edit icon
Tap the Delete icon for the map you want to delete
Tap Delete Map
