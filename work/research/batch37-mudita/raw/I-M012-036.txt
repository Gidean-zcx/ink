How to delete an alarm?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:35 AM
You can easily remove unwanted alarms on your Mudita Kompakt
Delete an alarm
Tap the clock on the Home screen.
Tap the pencil (edit) icon.
Tap the delete icon next to the alarm you want to remove.
Confirm the deletion if prompted.
Access the Alarm app
If the Alarm app icon is hidden, you can access it by navigating to: Apps → Alarm
From there, you can manage all alarms, including creating, editing, and deleting them
