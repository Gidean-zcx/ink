How To Use the Weather App on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:40 AM
How to open the weather app
Press the Control Center button
Tap Weather widget*
*You can also unhide the app icon to enable access by tapping Apps > Weather
How to change the weather default location
Open the Weather app
Tap the Menu icon
Tap the Pencil icon
Select the current default location
Either search for the location you want or tap Use your current location*
Tap Done
* You may need to manage location sharing in settings
