How to Import Contacts from Outlook to Your Kompakt?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:39 AM
You can easily transfer your contacts from Outlook to your Kompakt device using Mudita Center. Follow the steps below to complete the import process
Before importing your contacts, make sure that: 
Mudita Center is installed on your computer.
Your Kompakt device is connected to your computer.
You have access to your Outlook account credentials.
Importing Contacts from Outlook
Open Mudita Center and connect your Kompakt device.
In the left-hand menu, navigate to Your Kompakt and select Contacts.
Click Import contacts.
Choose Outlook as the contact source.
Sign in to your Outlook account when prompted.
Select the contacts you want to import, or choose All contacts to import your entire contact list.
Click Import selected contacts to start transferring the contacts to your phone.
Once the process is complete, your selected Outlook contacts will be available on your Kompakt device.
