How To Use the Camera and Gallery on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:41 AM
How to take a photo
Tap Apps
Tap Camera
Aim the camera at the object
Tap the photo button* 
*As Kompakt has an e-ink display, you may need to hold the phone still for a little longer than a typical smart phone
How to view photos
You can only view photos on Kompakt in greyscale but when you transfer your photos to a computer you can view them in full color.
Tap Apps
Tap Camera
Tap the Image icon
Tap the photo you want to view
How to take a screenshot
Press Power button and Volume down buttons at the same time*
*Currently there is no feedback that the screenshot was taken, but it will be there when you view your photos
