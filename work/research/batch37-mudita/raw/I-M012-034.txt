How to create an alarm?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:36 AM
You can create and customize alarms directly on your Mudita Kompakt
Create your first alarm:
Tap the clock on the Home screen.
Tap Create an Alarm.
Set the desired time.
(Optional) Edit the alarm name, sound, and repeat schedule.
Tap Create to save the alarm.
Access the Alarm app:
If the Alarm app icon is hidden, you can access it by navigating to: Apps → Alarm
From there, you can create, edit, enable, disable, or delete your alarms at any time
