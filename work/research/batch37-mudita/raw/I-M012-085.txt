Mudita Kompakt Possible SIM Card Configurations
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:38 AM
The Mudita Kompakt features a dual-SIM tray with one dedicated SIM card slot and another hybrid slot that can house either a second SIM card or MicroSD memory card. The phone can also have one eSIM phone number installed on it, which deactivates the second SIM card slot if used. However, a MicroSD card would still work, even with an eSIM number installed.
Possible SIM Configurations:
SIM (single or dual)
eSIM (single)
SIM + eSIM (one each)
SIM + SD card (one each)
eSIM + SD card (one each)
SIM + eSIM + SD card (one each)
Supported Card Formats:
SIM card format: nano-SIM (4FF)
SD card format: microSD
