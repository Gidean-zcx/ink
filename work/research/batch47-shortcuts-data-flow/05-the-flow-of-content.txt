The flow of content in Shortcuts on iPhone and iPad
When you run a shortcut, information is passed from action to action and transformed along the way. This information, or content, can be any type of data—text, photos, videos, URLs, Calendar events, songs, contacts, Health data, Maps locations, and much more.
Any number of items can be passed from one action to the next. Content is mostly invisible in the shortcut as it’s handed from action to action. And when a shortcut finishes, the content is discarded and the shortcut reset for the next time you run it.
