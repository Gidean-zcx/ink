Ground News helps readers compare how different news outlets report on the same story. By organizing coverage from across the political spectrum, we make it easier to understand how narratives can vary between sources.
Instead of relying on a single perspective, we provide tools that help readers explore the news from various viewpoints.
Compare News Coverage Across Sources
Ground News gathers reporting from thousands of news publishers and organizes them into a single coverage page for each story. By viewing multiple perspectives side-by-side, you can gain a broader understanding of how an event is being covered.
Our news browsing tools allow you to:
Compare headlines from multiple outlets
See how the same event is framed by different sources
Explore reporting from across the political spectrum
Learn more about our Sort & Filter tool to help you explore diverse perspectives and understand how different sources cover the same story.
Discover Blindspots in News Coverage
The ease of comparison makes it possible for our readers from all walks of life to "step into someone else's news" by viewing Blindspot stories, which highlight what news topics are being covered by only one side of the political spectrum.
Learn more about our Blindspot Feed here: https://ground.news/blindspot/about
The Blindspot Feed makes it straightforward for our readers to step outside of their personal news bubble and into someone else's, to gain a better understanding of what their political others may be reading.
Bias and Factuality Ratings
Ground News includes bias and factuality ratings, as well as source ownership information, for thousands of news sources across the globe. We gather this data from three independent organizations dedicated to assessing and rating bias in the news.
Our goal is never to dictate what to think and we keep our team’s subjective opinions out of the product in order to give our readers the most objective view of bias possible. Read all about our methodology here: https://ground.news/rating-system and the options available to you if you disagree with a bias rating.
