Yoto app (Beta)

Improved Card Linking: this process now happens within the app, not on a web page, so should be a much smoother and enjoyable experience.
Improved Player Setup options: you can now start this process from the device picker if you have not yet set up a Yoto Player. We made this change to give better options for people new to the app to do the thing they are likely to need to do first.
 Yoto App v2.10 (6th July 2020)
Track/Chapter Listings: see tracks and play from a specific track when your phone is selected as the play-on device (when Yoto Player is selected you can see tracks and play the content from the beginning). Navigate to tracks/chapters by tapping a card image or title in My Library or tapping the “Chapters” link on the maxi player.
New side bar navigation: on My Library for navigating between your library of content. This improves the navigation and paves the way for some exciting updates we have planned for the coming weeks.
How To Guides: available from updated Menu screen
Security update: see unverified members of your family account
Family Account organisation improved: Remove a family member from the family account (only when you are verified). Helpful if things got mixed up during setup.
