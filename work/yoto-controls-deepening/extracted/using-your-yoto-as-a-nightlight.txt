
Turn your Yoto Player onto its front, and the nightlight comes on. Stand it upright, and it’s off. But there’s a little more to it… 
One quick tip: flip it on a hard, flat surface like a bedside table. Avoid carpeted floors or beds.





Adjusting the brightness
While the player is face down, twist the right-hand orange dial - left to dim (all the way down to off), right to brighten.
Yoto Player (4th Generation) adjusts itself: its auto-brightness dims the nightlight to match the light in the room. Twisting the dial overrides it for the night - stand the player upright and pop it back down, and auto-brightness takes over again.


Changing the colour
Pick your nightlight colour in the Yoto App: go to the Settings tab and tap the arrow icon next to your player.
You can set a different colour for Day and Night mode - a lovely okay-to-wake cue for little ones who can’t read the clock yet: one colour means it’s still sleep time, another means it’s fine to get up. 





While the nightlight is on - Yoto Player (4th Generation)
The display switches off so it doesn’t light up the room. Your child can still:

Play and pause audio

Change the volume

Skip through tracks
Please also note that selecting content on the Green Button is disabled when the nightlight is on.


Good to know

Yoto Player (3rd Generation): the room-temperature sensor takes a break while the player is in nightlight mode.

Yoto Mini does not have a nightlight, but it does have a Day and Night mode!
