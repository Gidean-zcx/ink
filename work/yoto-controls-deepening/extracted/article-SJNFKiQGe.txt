Check the storage on an earlier Yoto Mini 
Turn it over and look at the serial number on the base:

16 GB - the serial number starts with 5 or a lower number, and doesn't end in -H, -H7 or -U.

32 GB - everything else (starts with 6 or higher, starts with letters, or ends in -H, -H7 or -U).


When the storage is full
Nothing you’ll notice - and don’t worry, it won’t be “lost” from your library: 

Your player automatically removes the audio that hasn’t been played for the longest time, to make room for new downloads of more popular content. 

Removed audio stays in your Yoto App library. To get it back on the player, just play it while your Yoto is online, it downloads again automatically.


Check storage and downloads
In the Yoto App, go to Settings, select your player and open Audio Download Status. If your model does not show a storage total, use the title list to check which audio is ready offline.
