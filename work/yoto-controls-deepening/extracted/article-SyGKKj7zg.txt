4th Generation controls


Left orange button

Turn to adjust the volume.

Short press to skip to the previous track.

Press and hold to rewind.

When the clock is showing, press and hold to pair Bluetooth headphones.




Right orange button

Turn to navigate through tracks, then press to select one.

Short press to skip to the next track.

Press and hold to fast-forward.




The Green Button

Twist to browse your child’s favourites - the artwork appears on the display - then press to play.

When nothing is playing and the clock is showing, press the Green Button to play the first item on the Green Button menu (default set to Yoto Daily). 

Note: A grown-up chooses what’s on the Green Button first, in the Yoto App. See: Using the Green Button. 


Power button – bottom-right corner on Yoto Player (4th Generation); top-right corner on Yoto Mini (4th Generation)

Long press to turn your player on or off.

Short press to check the battery level.


Earlier players controls


Left orange dial (Yoto Player and Yoto Mini)

Turn clockwise or anti-clockwise to adjust the volume.

Press to skip to the previous track or chapter (with a card in).

Press and hold (longer than 1 second) while something’s playing to rewind.

Press and hold for about 3 seconds with the clock showing to open the Bluetooth menu - then turn the right-hand dial to choose and press to confirm. [LINK PLACEHOLDER - Using headphones with your Yoto]


Right orange dial (Yoto Player and Yoto Mini)
With a card in:

Press to skip to the next track or chapter.

Turn to navigate forwards or backwards through tracks and chapters.

Press and hold (longer than 1 second) while something’s playing to fast-forward.
With no card in:

Press once to play Yoto Daily (when you’re online).

Press again for Yoto Radio - and once more to return to the clock.
Yoto Player only - nightlight mode (player face down):

Turn clockwise to brighten the nightlight (or turn it back on from zero), anti-clockwise to dim it down to zero.
Pro tip: you can customise what the right-hand dial does in day and night mode from the Yoto App - head to your player’s settings tab.


Power button
You’ll find it on the right side of Yoto Player (3rd Generation) and Yoto Mini, and in the bottom right-hand corner of Yoto Player (2nd Generation).

What happens

Short press
Pauses or resumes the audio - including audio playing from the app

Long press
Stops the audio

Hold for about 3 seconds
Turns the player on or off. On Yoto Player: the nightlight flashes twice as it turns on, and a white Yoto face appears as it turns off

Hold for 10 seconds
Hard reset - restarts a frozen or misbehaving player. Your Wi-Fi details and audio are safe
