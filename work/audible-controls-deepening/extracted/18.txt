L13: ## App keeps crashing
L14: 
L15:   * Aug 27, 2026
L16:   * Knowledge

L26: Answer
L27: 
L28: There are a few troubleshooting methods to try if the Audible app is consistently crashing (closing immediately after opening) or is slowly loading information.
L29: 
L30: In this article:
L31: 
L32:   * 【1†Force quit the app】
L33:   * 【2†Update the app】
L34:   * 【3†Clear app data and cache on Android】
L35:   * 【4†Re-install the app】
L36:   * 【5†Temporary fixes】
L37: ## Force quit the app
L38: 
L39:   1. Press the home button twice or swipe up from the bottom of your screen.
L40:   2. Close the Audible app.
L41:   3. Reopen the Audible app.
L42: 
L43: If the issue continues, you may need to update or reinstall your Audible app.
L44: ## Update the app
L45: 
L46: Occasionally, app features and functions may stop working because the app hasn't been updated to the latest version.
L47: 
L48: Note: An update will only display if there's an update available to download. If you're using an older device that doesn't support the latest version of the app, you can continue to listen to all of your audiobooks on another compatible device or in the Audible Cloud Player.
L49: ### Update the iOS app
L50: 
L51:   1. Open the App Store.
L52:   2. Select your profile icon.
L53:   3. Find and select the Audible app.
L54:   4. Select Update.
L55:   5. Select Open to view the latest version of the Audible app.
L56: 
L57: Note: If you don't see the Audible app, you may need to swipe down to refresh your list of available updates.
L58: 
L59: ### Update the Android app
L60: 
L61:   1. Open the Google Play Store.
L62:   2. Select your profile icon.
L63:   3. Select My apps & games.
L64:   4. Find the Audible app in the Updates pending list.
L65:   5. Select Update.
L66: ## Clear app data and cache on Android
L67: 
L68:   1. Go to the settings on your Android device.
L69:   2. Select Apps.
L70:   3. Find Audible in the list of all apps.
L71:   4. Select Storage or Storage & cache.
L72:   5. Select Clear Cache.
L73:   6. Select Clear Storage and confirm.
L74: ## Re-install the app
L75: 
L76:   1. Delete the app from your device.
L77:   2. Open your app store.
L78:   3. Find the Audible app.
L79:   4. Download the app.
L80: 
L81: Note: Uninstalling the app will remove any downloaded titles and those marked as "Finished." You may re-download these titles at any time and remark as "Finished."
L82: ## Temporary fixes
L83: 
L84: If none of the above solves the crashing problem, you can try the following to minimize disruptions:
L85: 
L86:   * Keeping the Audible app in the foreground when listening.
L87:   * Not switching between apps frequently.
L88:   * Closing all other apps when using Audible.
