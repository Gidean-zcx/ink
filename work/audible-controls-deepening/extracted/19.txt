L28: You can listen to Audible on your laptop or computer with the Audible Cloud Player. Since there's no 【1†Audible app for PC】, the Cloud Player makes it easy to enjoy your titles on Windows, Mac, or any other computer.
L29: 
L30: Why you'll love the Cloud Player:
L31:   * Pick up right where you left off 【2†across all your devices】.
L32:   * Start listening right away — no downloads needed.
L33:   * No extra software to install.
L34: 
L35: What you need is the latest version of one of these browsers:
L36: 
L37:   * Google Chrome
L38:   * Mozilla Firefox
L39:   * Apple Safari
L40:   * Microsoft Edge
L41: 
L42: Note: The Audible Cloud Player lets you stream and listen to your titles as long as you're connected to the internet. To listen offline, visit 【3†Download titles】.

L48: ## How do I listen to a title on my desktop browser?
L49: 
L50:   1. Open the 【7†Audible†www.audible.com】 site and sign in.
L51:   2. Select 【8†Library†www.audible.com】 from the site's navigation.
L52:   3. Select Listen now next to the title of your choosing.
L53:   4. The Cloud Player will appear.
L54: ## How do I listen on my mobile browser?
L55: 
L56: You can also use the Cloud Player on your phone or tablet's mobile browser.
L57: 
L58:   1. Go to 【9†Audible.com†audible.com】 from your mobile device
L59:   2. Select the main menu
L60:   3. Select Library
L61:   4. Select the title you want to listen to
L62: 
L63: The Cloud Player will appear.
L64: ## How to fix the Cloud Player when it won’t load?
L65: 
L66: If your Cloud Player doesn't load when you try to listen on a browser, it may be due to an issue with your browsing history.
L67: 
L68: Note: make sure you're using the latest version of your browser.
L69: ### How to clear your history in Google Chrome:
L70: 
L71:   1. Select Chrome from the main navigation.
L72:   2. Select Clear Browsing Data.
L73:   3. Select the Advanced tab.
L74:   4. From the Time range drop-down menu, select All time.
L75:   5. Check the following boxes:
L76:     1. Browsing history
L77:     2. Download history
L78:     3. Cookies and other site data
L79:     4. Cached images and files
L80:   6. Select Clear data.
L81: ### How to clear your history in Safari:
L82: 
L83:   1. Select History from the main navigation.
L84:   2. Select Clear History.
L85:   3. From the Clear drop-down menu, select all history.
L86:   4. Select Clear History.
L87: ### How to clear your history in Firefox:
L88: 
L89:   1. Select History in the main navigation.
L90:   2. Select Clear Recent History.
L91:   3. Change the option from “Last hour” to Everything in the “Time range to clear” drop-down menu.
L92:   4. Check the following boxes in the pop-up:
L93:     1. Browsing & download history
L94:     2. Cookies
L95:     3. Cache
L96:     4. Form & search history
L97:   5. Select OK.
