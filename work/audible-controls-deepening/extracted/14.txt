L28: Control how titles play automatically in the Audible app. You can have your next selected title play automatically or discover related titles through previews when your current listen ends. For additional player options, please visit 【1†View player settings】.

L35: ## How do I turn on Up next titles?
L36: 
L37: You can have your selected titles play automatically without interruption.
L38: 
L39:   1. Go to your Profile.
L40:   2. Select the Settings gear icon.
L41:   3. Select Player.
L42:   4. Scroll to Continuous listening.
L43:   5. Toggle Up next titles on.
L44: ## How do I add titles to my Up next list?
L45: 
L46: You can add titles to your Up next list directly from your Library. This feature is only available in the app.
L47: 
L48: Using the 3-dot More menu:
L49: 
L50:   1. Go to your Library.
L51:   2. Select your title's 3-dot More menu.
L52:   3. Select Play next.
L53: 
L54: Using select and hold:
L55: 
L56:   1. Open the Audible app.
L57:   2. Go to your Library.
L58:   3. Find the title you want to play next.
L59:   4. Select and hold on the title.
L60:   5. Select Play Next.
L61: ## How do I edit titles in my Up next list?
L62: 
L63:   1. Go to the player screen of your current listen.
L64:   2. Select the Chapters menu.
L65:   3. Select Up next.
L66:   4. Select the 3-dot More menu next to the title you want to edit.
L67:   5. Choose from the following options:
L68:      * Title details
L69:      * Share
L70:      * Move to top
L71:      * Move up
L72:      * Move down
L73:      * Move to bottom
L74:      * Remove from queue
L75: ## How do I turn on related title previews?
L76: 
L77: Discover new titles through automatic previews when your current listen ends.
L78: 
L79:   1. Go to your Profile.
L80:   2. Select the Settings gear icon.
L81:   3. Select Player.
L82:   4. Scroll to Continuous listening.
L83:   5. Toggle Recommended titles on.
L84: 
L85: Note:
L86: 
L87:   * Related title previews will only play if you don't have another title queued up to play next.
L88:   * You can toggle Recommended titles off if you wish to stop the auto-play of related titles.
