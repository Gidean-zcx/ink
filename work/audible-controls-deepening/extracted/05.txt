L26: Answer
L27: 
L28: You can save, edit, delete, and play specific clips of an audiobook right from the player in your app. Creating clips makes it easy to replay your favorite sections, quotes, or bits of dialogue. They can be as long as 44 seconds and as short as 5 seconds. You can also bookmark certain places you'd like to return to later, from both the app or Cloud Player.
L29: If you're looking for more information about Cloud Player, including how to use it and common troubleshooting techniques, please refer to 【1†Listen on a browser.】
L30: 
L31: Notes:
L32: 
L33:   * Any clip you save in the app will also add a bookmark.
L34:   * Select Go to Bookmark from the app or 【1†Cloud Player】 will resume playback from that exact position.
L35:   * You aren't able to add or manage clips from the website—only the app.

L47: ## Save an audio clip
L48: 
L49: The Audio Clips feature is exclusively accessible through the Audible app on Android and iOS devices.
L50: 
L51: In the app
L52: 
L53:   1. Open the title's player screen.
L54:   2. Select the +Clip icon to automatically save 30 seconds.
L55: ## Edit an audio clip
L56: 
L57: You can edit and access the Audio Clip features through the Audible app.
L58: 
L59: In the app
L60: 
L61: From the confirmation banner
L62: 
L63:   1. Open the title's player screen.
L64:   2. Select the +Clip icon to automatically save 30 seconds.
L65:   3. Select Edit on the confirmation banner “Clip Saved.”
L66:   4. Drag the audio clip handles to set your desired beginning and end point.
L67: 
L68: From Clips & Notes
L69:   1. Open the title's player screen.
L70:   2. Select the Clips & Notes button.
L71:   3. Find the clip you'd like to edit and select the 3-dot menu.
L72:   4. Select Edit Clip.
L73:   5. Drag the audio clip handles to set your desired beginning and end point.

L83:   2. Select the +Clip icon to automatically save 30 seconds.

L85:   4. Enter your note.

L88: From Clips & Notes

L90:   2. Select the Clips & Notes button.
L91:   3. Find the clip you'd like to edit and select the 3-dot menu.

L93:   5. Enter your note.

L106: ## Play a saved audio clip
L107: 
L108: You can play and save audio clips through the Audio Clips feature through the Audible app.
L109: 
L110: In the app
L111: 
L112:   1. Open the title's player screen.
L113:   2. Select the Clips & Notes button.
L114:   3. Find the clip you'd like to play.

L116: ## Add a bookmark
L117: 
L118: You can add and access podcast bookmarks in the Audible app or Cloud Player.
L119: 
L120: In the app
L121: 
L122:   1. Open the podcast episode.
L123:   2. Select +Bookmark.
L124: 
L125: On Cloud Player
L126: 
L127:   1. Open a title's player screen.
L128:   2. Select Add a bookmark.
L129: ## Add a note to a bookmark
L130: 
L131: You can add notes to your bookmarks through the Cloud Player and the Audible app.
L132: 
L133: In the app
L134: 
L135: From the confirmation banner
L136: 
L137:   1. Open the podcast episode.
L138:   2. Select +Bookmark.

L140:   4. Enter your note.
L141:   5. Select Save.
L142: 
L143: From Clips & Notes
L144:   1. Open the podcast episode.
L145:   2. Select the Bookmarks & Notes button.
L146:   3. Select the 3-dots More menu.
L147:   4. Select Add note.
L148:   5. Enter your note.
L149:   6. Select Save.
L150: 
L151: On Cloud Player
L152: 
L153: From the confirmation pop-up
L154: 
L155:   1. Open a title's player screen.
L156:   2. Select Add a bookmark.
L157:   3. Select Add note on the pop-up screen.
L158:   4. Select Save.
