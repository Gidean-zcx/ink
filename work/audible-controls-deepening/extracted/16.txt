L28: The Audible Listen Log is a feature that tracks your listening stats and activity. It shows you everything from when you first started a title to when you finished listening to it, including dates and times.

L36: ## What is the Listen Log?
L37: 
L38: Your Listen Log is a day-to-day record of your listening activity for a particular title. You can see the date and time you started listening, when you paused, and how long your listening session lasted.
L39: 
L40: Notes:
L41: 
L42:   * The Listen Log displays both dates and times for each session.
L43:   * Listen Log data cannot be downloaded or exported.
L44: ## What is the purpose of the Listen Log?
L45: 
L46: The Listen Log can help you find your place if you get lost. It's a very useful tool if, for example:
L47: 
L48:   * you fell asleep while listening and the book advanced without you;
L49:   * you went back to an earlier chapter to re-listen to something, but didn't note where you had left off;
L50:   * you accidentally moved the scrubber bar in the player and the book is now in a different section altogether.
L51: Using the Listen Log, you can get back to the starting point of your last intentional listening section.
L52: ## How do I find my Listen Log?
L53: 
L54: The Listen Log is available only on the Audible app and can be accessed through these steps:
L55: 
L56:   1. Open the Audible app.
L57:   2. Open the player for a title you're currently enjoying or already finished.
L58:   3. Select the Listen Log button below the cover of the book.
L59: 
L60: From here, you can choose to replay a section or clear your entire history.
L61: ## How do I view my Listening History?
L62: 
L63: Your Listening History shows all the titles you've listened to over time.
L64: 
L65: In the app
L66: 
L67:   1. Open the Audible app.
L68:   2. Go to your Profile.
L69:   3. Swipe down to Activity & Stats.
L70:   4. Select Listening History.
L71: 
L72: On desktop
L73: 
L74:   1. Go to the Audible site.
L75:   2. Select your name on the top navigation.
L76:   3. Select Listen History.
L77: ## How do I remove a title from my Listening History?
L78: 
L79: On desktop
L80: 
L81:   1. Go to the Audible site.
L82:   2. Select your name on the top navigation.
L83:   3. Select Listen History.
L84:   4. Select Remove next to the title you want to remove.
L85: 
L86: Note: Listening History data cannot be downloaded or exported.
