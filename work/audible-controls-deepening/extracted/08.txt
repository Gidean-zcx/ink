L38: ## Eligibility requirements
L39: 
L40:   * You must be an active 【1†Audible Premium】 member in good standing.
L41:   * You're making the return within 365 days of its purchase date.
L42:   * You purchased the title with an Audible credit.
L43: Note: We reserve the right to limit the number of returns or revoke return privileges at our discretion. Based on your return history, the self-service refund option may not be available. Audible may cancel or modify the terms of our returns option with respect to any or all participants at any time. Customers who cancel or fail to maintain their membership in good standing are not eligible to make returns. This return option doesn't affect any rights you have under consumer law.
L44: Returns may only be made through the same method in which you purchased the title. For example, if you purchased a title through the Google Play Store or App Store, you can only return that title through the Google Play Store or App Store customer service.
L45: ## How do I return an audiobook on mobile?
L46: 
L47: Return Audible titles from your iOS device or Android device by following these steps:
L48: 
L49:   1. Go to 【6†www.audible.com†www.audible.com】 and sign in to your account.
L50:   2. Select the main menu.
L51:   3. Select your name.
L52:   4. Select 【7†Purchase history†www.audible.com】.
L53:   5. Find the title you want to return and select it.
L54:   6. Select Return this title and confirm.
L55: Note: Refunded 【8†credits】 will expire 12 months after being issued, regardless of the original credit type. After confirming your return, 1 credit will be added back to your account.

L64: ## What happens to your Audible title when you return its companion Kindle eBook?
L65: When you purchase an Audible title at a discounted price through the "Add Narration" offer (which offers savings when you purchase an audiobook alongside its Kindle eBook), the two titles are linked. If you later make an eligible return of that Kindle eBook, Audible will automatically process a refund for the Audible title and remove it from your Library. This refund will be issued to your original payment method, and you will receive a notification when this action occurs.
