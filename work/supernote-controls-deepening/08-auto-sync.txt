Supernote Cloud Auto Sync is a powerful feature that keeps your files consistent across devices, up to date and readily available when you need them. Learn how to harness the full potential of Supernote Cloud Auto Sync and unlock a seamless file management experience.
Swipe down from the top of the screen and tap
Settings
in the top menu bar.
Tap
Drive
.
Log in to your Supernote Account: If you haven't already logged in, enter your Supernote account credentials to proceed. Skip this step if you are already logged in.
Toggle
Auto Sync
to ON.
Swipe down from the top of the screen again to view the syncing progress.
