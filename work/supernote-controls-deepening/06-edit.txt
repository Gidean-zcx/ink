The ability to edit content is essential for effective note-taking, as it gives you control over how you organize and structure your notes. In this article, we will explain how to edit headings (originally named titles) and links.
Edit headings
Once a heading is created, you can re-lasso it to enter the edit mode and perform the following editing actions:
Move the heading where you need it to be by dragging it with pen to the desired spot on the current page where it exists.
Zoom in or out of the heading by dragging any of the four corners of the rectangle outward or inward.
Copy the heading by tapping
, and then paste it to anywhere on the current page where it exists by single-tapping the screen with pen.
Copy the heading to another page of the same notebook by tapping
, and then paste it by single-tapping the screen with pen.
Change the heading style by tapping the Headings icon and selecting the heading style you want to use.
Turn the heading into a keyword by tapping the keyword icon.
Turn the heading into a calendar event title by tapping the calendar icon.
Edit links
Similarly, after you create a link, you can re-lasso it to perform the same editing actions:
Move the link where you need it to be by dragging it with pen to the right spot on the current page where it exists.
Zoom in or out of the link by dragging any of the four corners of the rectangle outward or inward.
Copy the link by tapping
, and then paste it to anywhere on the current page where it exists by single-tapping the screen with pen.
Copy the link to another page of the same notebook by tapping
, and then paste it by single-tapping the screen with pen.
Turn the link into a keyword by tapping the keyword icon.
Turn the link into a calendar event title by tapping the calendar icon.
Tap the “Link” icon
and then tap
to change the link attributes, including the link style and target, or tap
to cancel/delete the link.
