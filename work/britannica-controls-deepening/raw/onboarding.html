    FAQ and Troubleshooting

    Access & Accounts

    Why can’t I save articles or create resource packs?

    Saving content and organizing it into resource packs requires a personal My Britannica account. If you are not signed in, the Save option will not store content.

    Why are my students being asked to log in repeatedly?

    This usually happens when authentication cookies are being cleared by browser settings or network security software. If cookies are deleted regularly, Britannica cannot maintain the login session. Your IT team may need to allow Britannica authentication cookies on network devices.

    Why can’t I see sharing options like Google Classroom or Microsoft Teams?

    Sharing tools depend on your school’s subscription and account permissions. If these options are not visible, your institution may not have them enabled or they may be restricted by account settings.

    Why don’t some features work after translating an article?

    Translation uses Google Translate to generate a machine-translated version of the article. Because this version is not part of Britannica’s original content system, tools such as Cite, Save, Send, or Read-Aloud may be disabled.

    Why can’t I create or edit lesson plans?

    Lesson plan tools require a personal My Britannica account and signing in as an educator. If you are not signed in, the lesson planning tools will not appear.

    Research & Classroom Use

    Why are some topics available in only one reading level?

    Many topics are available at multiple reading levels, but some specialized subjects may have only one version.

    Why can’t my students access Primary Sources or E-Books?

    Primary Sources and E-Books are available only in the Middle and High levels of Britannica School. Students accessing the Early Elementary and Elementary levels will not see these resources.

    Why can’t students see the same reading level I selected?

    Reading level selection is controlled individually by each user. Changing the level adjusts the content only for that user.

    How can I help students cite Britannica content correctly?

    Every article, image, and video includes a built-in citation tool that generates MLA, APA, Chicago, or Harvard citations.

    How can I quickly find articles aligned to curriculum standards?

    From an article page, open the related section, and select the Teacher option to view correlated curriculum standards.

    Technical Issues

    What browsers work best with Britannica School?

    For the best performance, use the latest version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Microsoft Edge.

    Why aren’t images, videos, or interactives loading?

    Possible causes include slow Internet connections, outdated browsers, or network filtering.

    Why won’t a PDF or document open?

    Some resources require a PDF reader. If one is not installed, your browser may prompt you to download one.

    Why doesn’t the dictionary tool appear for certain words?

    The dictionary works by double-clicking a word within article text. It may not work on headings, images, or interactive media.

    Why does printing look different from the article page?

    For best results, use the Print icon within the article toolbar, which opens a formatted print version including a citation.

    Product & Support

    Where can I find educator resources for using Britannica School?

    Educators can access teaching tools, lesson resources, and instructional strategies through Teach Britannica in the main navigation.

    What should I do if my school cannot access Britannica School?

    Contact your school librarian, administrator, or IT department to confirm your institution’s subscription and network configuration.
