L18: # How do I delete an app and re-download?
L19: 
L20:   * February 27, 2026 00:11
L21:   * Updated
L22: ## Apple iOS devices
L23:   * To delete the application from your device, press down on the app icon you want to delete until all of the icons start shaking
L24:   * An X should appear on the top corner of the icons
L25:   * Press the X of the app icon you want to delete
L26:   * Open the App Store, search for the app, then re-download the application with the same iTunes login
L27:   * If you are re-downloading Khan Academy Kids, no payment is necessary since Khan Academy Kids is completely free to download
L28:   * Please restart your device after re-downloading (see "【13†How to I restart my device】")
L29: ## Android devices
L30: 
L31:   * Open Settings on your device
L32:   * Tap on Apps or Application manager
L33:   * Tap on the app you want to remove (you may need to scroll to find the right one)
L34:   * Tap Uninstall
