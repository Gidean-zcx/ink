L18: # How do I mute the background music?
L19: 
L20:   * February 26, 2026 23:52
L21:   * Updated
L22: 
L23: Background music plays during the user experience in Khan Academy Kids. To mute the background music, tap the gray music note in the bottom right corner of the Home screen so that the icon is crossed out.
