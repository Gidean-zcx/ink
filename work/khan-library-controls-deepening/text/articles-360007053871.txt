L18: # How do I change the avatar for a user?
L19: 
L20:   * February 27, 2026 00:12
L21:   * Updated
L22: 
L23: 【13†Image: IMG_0240.PNG】
L24: 
L25: Editing a user’s avatar can be managed from the Parent section inside Khan Academy Kids.
L26: 
L27: To edit a user’s avatar:
L28:   * Open the Khan Academy Kids app on your device
L29:   * Tap “For Parents” at the bottom of the opening page of the app (or select the user avatar in the top right corner of the Home screen)
L30:   * Follow instructions to access the “Grown-Ups Only” section
L31:   * Tap a user to, and edit the user’s avatar under My Account
