L18: # How do I delete a user?
L19: 
L20:   * February 27, 2026 00:12
L21:   * Updated
L22: 
L23: To delete a user:
L24: 
L25:   * Open the Khan Academy Kids app on your device
L26:   * Tap “For Parents” at the bottom of the opening page of the app (or select the user avatar in the top right corner of the Home screen)
L27:   * Follow instructions to access the “Grown-Ups Only” section
L28:   * Select the gray Enable Delete button under the email address listed for My Account
L29:   * Tap the X on the user avatar to delete the user profile
L30: Please note that deleting a user profile will erase the progress and learning path history for that user.
