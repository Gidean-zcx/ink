How to Send an MMS Message to a Contact?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
Sending an MMS message on your Kompakt lets you quickly share photos with your contacts. Simply select a recipient, attach an image, and send your message
Open the SMS app.
Tap the Plus icon to create a new message.
Select the contact you want to message.
Tap the Image icon to attach a photo.
Select the image you want to send.
Tap Send to send your MMS message.
