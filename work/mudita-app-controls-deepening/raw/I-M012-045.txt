How to listen to a voice recording
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:35 AM
Listening to a voice recording on your device is quick and easy. Follow the steps below to access and play your recordings 
Tap Apps
Tap Recorder
Tap the Menu icon
Select the recording you want to listen to
Tap the Play button to start playback
Tap Done to close the recording screen
