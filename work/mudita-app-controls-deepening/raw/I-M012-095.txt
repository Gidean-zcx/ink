How To Manage Mudita Kompakt Contacts With Mudita Center
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:47 AM
How to import contacts from CSV file or vCard
Before you start, you need to create a CSV using outlook, create a vCard using Google Account or create a vCard (VCF file) using iCloud.
Open Mudita Center and connect your device
Look under Your Kompakt > Click Contacts
Click Import contacts
Click Upload CSV or vCard
Select the CSV or Vcard file from your computer
Select the individual contacts to import or select All contacts
Click Import selected contacts to transfer contacts to phone*
*Important - do not disconnect your phone while the import is in progress
How to import contacts from Google
Open Mudita Center and connect your device
Look under Your Kompakt > Click Contacts
Click Import contacts
Click Google
Log in to Google
Select the individual contacts to import or select All contacts
Click Import selected contacts to transfer contacts to phone*
*Important - do not disconnect your phone while the import is in progress 
How to import contacts from Outlook
Open Mudita Center and connect your device
Look under Your Kompakt > Click Contacts
Click Import contacts
Click Outlook
Log in to Outlook
Select the individual contacts to import or select All contacts
Click Import selected contacts to transfer contacts to phone*
*Important - do not disconnect your phone while the import is in progress 
How to create a vCard (VCF file) using iCloud
On your computer, sign into your iCloud account using your Apple ID 
Click Contacts
Select the contacts to export either by using ctrl + click or shift + click*
Click the Export Icon
Click Export vCard
The vCard (VCF file) should appear in your downloads folder
*To quickly select all contacts, click the 3 dots in a circle icon > click Select All
How to create a CSV or vCard using Google Account
On your computer, sign into your Gmail account using your Google ID 
Click the Grid icon to show the apps list 
Click Contacts
Select the contacts to export 
Click the 3 dots icon
Click Export 
Select how you would like to export the contacts, either CSV or vCard
Click Export 
The CSV or vCard should appear in your downloads folder
*To quickly select all contacts, click the down arrow icon > click All
How to create a CSV file using Outlook
On your computer, sign into your Outlook account using your Microsoft ID 
Click the People icon 
Click the Manage contacts
Click Export 
Click Export 
The CSV should appear in your downloads folder
How to delete contacts using Mudita Center
Open Mudita Center and connect your device
Look under Your Kompakt > Click Contacts
Hover the mouse pointer over contact you want to delete
Click the Checkbox to select the contact* 
Select any other contacts you want to delete
Click Delete
Click Delete
*To select all, click the minus icon near the top of the screen
