How To Manage Contacts on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:44 AM
How to manually add contacts on Kompakt
Tap Phone
Tap Contacts
Tap the Add contact icon
Enter the contact information
Tap Create
How to delete contacts on Kompakt
Tap Phone 
Tap Contacts
Select the contact you want to delete
Tap the Pencil icon
Tap the Bin icon
Tap Delete contact
How to block contacts on Kompakt
Tap Phone
Tap Contacts
Select the contact you want to block
Tap the Pencil icon
Tap the Block icon
Tap Block contact
How to unblock contacts on Kompakt
Tap Phone
Tap Contacts
Select the contact you want to block
Tap the Pencil icon
Tap the Block icon
Tap Unblock contact
