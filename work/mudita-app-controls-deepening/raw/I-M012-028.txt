How to Send an MMS Message to a New Number?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:38 AM
Sending an MMS message to a new number on your Kompakt is quick and easy. You can attach a photo and send it even if the recipient is not saved in your contacts
Open the SMS app.
Tap the Plus icon to create a new message.
Enter the recipient's phone number.
Tap Send to: [phone number] to confirm the recipient.
Tap the Image icon to attach a photo.
Select the image you want to send.
Tap Send to send your MMS message.
