How to Send SMS Messages to a Contact?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
Sending SMS messages on your Kompakt is simple and allows you to quickly start conversations with your contacts or new numbers
Tap SMS.
Tap the Pencil icon to create a new message.
Select the contact you want to message.
Enter your message in the text field.
Tap the Send icon to send the message.
If the keyboard does not appear, tap the blinking cursor to activate it.
