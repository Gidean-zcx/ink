How To Add and Delete Fingerprints on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:42 AM
How to add fingerprints
Kompakt’s power button contains a fingerprint scanner, letting you unlock you phone quickly without the passcode.
To add a new fingerprint:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Security
Tap Manage Fingerprints
Type your passcode and tap Enter on the keypad
Tap the Plus icon
Follow the onscreen instructions*
*Make sure you place a slightly different part of your finger on the sensor after each vibration
How to delete fingerprints
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Security
Tap Manage Fingerprints
Type your passcode and tap Enter on the keypad
Tap the Bin icon next to the fingerprint you want to delete
Tap Delete
