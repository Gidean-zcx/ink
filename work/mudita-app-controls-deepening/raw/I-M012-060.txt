How To Change the Screen Time Out Delay on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:35 AM
To save power and to keep your phone secure, Kompakt’s screen will sleep after set time delay.
To change the screen timeout delay:
Press the Control Center button
Tap Settings icon
Tap Display
Tap Screen Timeout
Select the time delay you want
