How to Delete Contacts Using Mudita Center?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:37 AM
You can easily delete contacts from your Kompakt using Mudita Center
Follow the steps below:
Open Mudita Center and connect your Kompakt to your computer.
Under Your Kompakt, click Contacts.
Hover your mouse over the contact you want to delete.
Click the checkbox to select the contact.
Select any additional contacts you want to remove.
Click Delete.
Confirm by clicking Delete again.
Tip: To select all contacts at once, click the minus icon checkbox near the top of the the screen
