How to Transfer Music to Kompakt?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:36 AM
You can easily transfer music to your Kompakt using Mudita Center. Follow the steps below to upload your audio files
Open Mudita Center and connect your Kompakt to your computer.
Under Your Kompakt, click Manage Files.
Select the Music tab.
Click Add file.
Browse your computer and select the audio files you want to upload.
Confirm the transfer to copy the selected music files to your Kompakt.
Important: If a file format is not supported by Kompakt, you won't be able to select or upload it
