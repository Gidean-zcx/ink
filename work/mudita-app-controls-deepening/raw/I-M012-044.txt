How to record a voice recording?
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:36 AM
To record a voice recording on your device, follow these steps:
Tap Apps
Tap Recorder
Tap the Record button to start recording
Tap the Pause button to pause the recording
Tap Done to stop recording
Tap New recording to add a title
Tap Done to save your recording
