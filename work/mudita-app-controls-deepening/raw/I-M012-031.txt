How To Create and Delete Notes on Mudita Kompakt
Modified on: Sat, 3 Oct, 2026 at 8:40 AM
How to create notes
Tap Apps
Tap Notes
Tap the Plus icon*
Write a title for your note (max. 54 characters)
Tap Enter on the keyboard
Write more detail for your note
Tap Done
*If it is your first note, tap Create new note
How to delete notes
Tap Apps
Tap Notes
Tap the Edit icon
Tap the Check box to select the note(s) you want to delete
Tap Delete
Tap Delete
