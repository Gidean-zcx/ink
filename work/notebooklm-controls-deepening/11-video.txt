Generate Video Overviews in Gemini Notebook
Important: At this time, the Gemini Notebook mobile app may have limitations to this feature. Learn more about the supported features in Gemini Notebook mobile app.
Video Overviews transform the sources in your notebook into an engaging video. They distill complex information into clear, digestible content, providing a comprehensive and engaging visual deep dive of your material.
Generate video overviews
Important: Video overviews, including voices and visuals, are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies or audio glitches.
On your device, open Gemini Notebook.
Open an existing notebook or create a new one with sources.
Learn how to add or discover new sources for your notebook.
In the "Studio" panel, select Video Overview.
Customize your video overview.
Learn how to customize your Video Overview.
Select Generate now or Generate later.
Gemini Notebook may take a while to generate the Video Overview (sometimes, more than 30 minutes). Feel free to come back to your notebook later.
Co-create video overviews with Gemini Notebook
Important:
This feature is only available for Google AI Ultra subscribers.
Video overviews, including voices and visuals, are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies or audio glitches.
Step 1: Open a notebook you have edit access to
On your device, open Gemini Notebook.
Open an existing notebook or create a new one with sources.
Learn how to add or discover new sources for your notebook.
Step 2: Create your producer notes
From the “Studio” panel
In the "Studio" panel, select Video Overview.
Customize your video overview.
Learn how to customize your Video Overview.
Turn on Review producer notes.
Select Generate now.
Tip: If you select Generate later, you’ll schedule a generation of the video overview without producer notes.
From Gemini Notebook chat
In the chat box, enter a prompt about creating a video overview.
For example: Create a video summarizing [topic]
Customize your video overview through the responsive cards that Gemini Notebook returns to you in chat.
Turn on Review producer notes before final rendering.
Select Generate.
Gemini Notebook may take multiple minutes to generate producer notes. You can continue working in your notebook in the meantime.
Step 3: Generate your video overview
In the “Studio” panel, next to producer notes that are ready, select Review.
To refine your producer notes, add all of your feedback at once.
Select Proceed.
Important: Once you select Proceed, Gemini Notebook will generate your video overview. You will not be able to further refine your producer notes.
Gemini Notebook may take a while to generate the Video Overview (sometimes, more than 30 minutes). Feel free to come back to your notebook later.
(Optional) Customize your Video Overview
To customize your Video Overviews, specify the following:
Format: Choose the type of video that works best for you.
Cinematic: A rich, immersive experience that can unpack the complex ideas of your sources through engaging visuals and storytelling.
Important: Cinematic Overviews are only available to users ages 18+.
Explainer: A structured, comprehensive overview that connects the dots within your sources.
Short: Quickly grasp key concepts from your sources in ~60 second videos.
Language: Pick from any of our supported languages for your video's narration.
Cinematic Video Overviews only support English.
Learn how to set your output language for Video Overviews.
Visual Style: Select a look for your video (only for 18+ users, excluding Cinematic and Short Video Overviews). You can choose styles in these ways:
Choose from styles like Classic, Whiteboard, Watercolor, Retro Print, Heritage, Paper-craft, Kawaii, and Anime.
Let Gemini Notebook auto-select a style for you.
To generate a custom visual style, choose Custom and input a description.
(Optional) Steering prompt: Select a suggested topic for the overview to focus on, or provide a custom topic.
Manage your Video Overview
To change the playback speed, select a different speed from “1x”.
Navigate forward or backwards in the video by using the playback slider, or by tapping “rewind” or “skip forward”.
Enter full screen mode by clicking on the full screen button
To send feedback, tap “good video” or “bad video”
Play a Video Overview
Important: This is only applicable to notebooks that have previously generated Video Overviews.
Open an existing notebook.
In the "Studio" panel, select the title of your previously generated Video Overview.
Once the video player opens, start the Video Overview by hitting “play”
Tip: to view the prompt used to generate your audio overview, select “View custom prompt” within the three dots menu next to your artifact in the Studio panel.
Share a Video Overview
Option 1: Share a link
Generate a Video Overview.
In the video player, select Share.
Confirm that the notebook is shared with the recipient or is made public (open to “Anyone with a link”), and viewers have access to the “full notebook.”
Copy link to Video Overview to share with recipient.
Save to apply changes.
Important:
Public notebook sharing is only enabled for consumer accounts. It's currently disabled for Workspace Enterprise or Education accounts.
Only notebook owners and editors can make a  generated video publicly available and manage its access. As an owner or editor, you can turn off public access by selecting a different Notebook access level in the sharing panel.
Once a video is deleted, any previously generated share link will no longer work.
Option 2: Share the entire notebook
Generate a Video Overview.
Share the full notebook.
Other users can access the Video Overview in the "Studio" panel.
Option 3: Download & share the Video Overview
To download the Video Overview, select Download .
Share the downloaded video file.
Tip: Depending on the publisher's content restrictions, you may not be able to download artifacts generated using a notebook that has a Play Books ebook as a source.
