Generate reports in Gemini Notebook
Generate interactive reports with embedded studio content or structured, text-only documents in Gemini Notebook.
Important: Reports are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies or mistakes. Be sure to review generated content for accuracy.
Generate interactive reports in Gemini Notebook
Important: This feature is only available on the web. You must be 18 years of age or older to access some content in interactive reports.
Generate interactive learning overviews
Go to notebook.google.com.
Select a notebook. You can:
Open an existing Gemini Notebook that you have edit access to.
Create a new one and upload sources.
In the “Studio” panel, select Reports  Interactive.
To generate with default settings, under “Template,” select Learning Overview.
Learn how to customize your generation settings.
In your generated report, under any suggested artifacts that you want to embed, select Add.
You can also find the generated artifacts in the studio panel.
Tip: It may take some time to generate interactive reports and artifacts.
Generate document reports in Gemini Notebook
Go to notebook.google.com.
Select a notebook. You can:
Open an existing Gemini Notebook that you have edit access to.
Create a new one and upload sources.
In the “Studio” panel, select Reports  Document.
To generate with default settings, select a template or suggested format.
Learn how to customize your generation settings.
Customize your generation settings
Important: This feature is only available on the web.
Next to the name of the template or suggested format, select Edit .
Make your changes.
Select Generate now or Generate later.
Share your Gemini Notebook reports
Share a link to your report
Important: Make sure your notebook is already shared with the recipient or open to anyone with a link, and users have access to the full notebook.
Go to notebook.google.com.
In the “Studio” panel of an existing notebook, select your generated report.
Select Share   Copy link to Report.
Share your entire notebook
If you share the notebook that contains your generated reports with others, they can find those reports in the "Studio" panel.
Learn how to share notebooks privately.
Learn how to share a public notebook.
