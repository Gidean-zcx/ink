My question wasn't answered
Sometimes Gemini Notebook can’t answer your question because of:
Safety flags: The content of your source contains language that triggers a safety flag. This occurs with sensitive topics like violence, sexuality, or obscenity, even in historical contexts.
Unclear phrases: When your notebook contains many sources, Gemini Notebook retrieves the most relevant information based on your question, then builds a response from it. A specific question helps Gemini Notebook locate the most relevant pieces of information.
Tip: Make your question more precise and understandable.
Information not in sources: Gemini Notebook answers questions based on the information provided in your uploaded sources. If the answer isn't in the source material, it won't provide a response.
How to report offensive responses generated in Gemini Notebook
If you think a response generated in Gemini Notebook is offensive or unsafe, let us know.
To report a response, select thumbs down   Offensive/unsafe  Submit.
We’ll review the report and take appropriate actions to improve the safety of our model.
Why does the share menu show a lock icon?
While a notebook isn't shared with anyone the lock icon will appear indicating the notebook is private. It changes to the shared icon when the notebook is shared individually with users, and a globe icon when the notebook is publicly shared.
How to report a privately shared Notebook for my infringed rights
If you find content in a privately shared notebook that violates the law, let us know by either opening the settings menu in your notebook and selecting Report Notebook or by visiting this direct link.
We’ll review the material and consider if we’ll block, limit, or remove access to it.
Tip: You can also report through report content on Google.

Your data will never be shared by Gemini Notebook. Learn how Gemini Notebook protects your data.
File size limit for sources in Gemini Notebook
The current limit is 500,000 words per source or up to 200MB for local uploads. There’s no page limit.
My source import failed
Sources can’t be imported into Gemini Notebook if:
It exceeds the allowed 500,000 word limit per source.
The file size exceeds the allowed 200MB limit.
Your original PDF file is copy-protected.
How sources, chat context, and notes are used in prompts
Notes: Only when you specifically select it.
Sources: Always used in either the entire set or the subset you select.
Conversation history: Used to generate responses.
I don't always get citations
If your source content is too short, Gemini Notebook references the entire document without a cited individual text from your source.
Recover deleted notes in Gemini Notebook
