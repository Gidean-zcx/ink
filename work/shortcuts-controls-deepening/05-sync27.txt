Sync shortcuts on iPhone or iPad
If you’re signed in to your Apple Account on your Apple devices and turn on iCloud Sync in the Shortcuts app, you can keep your collection of shortcuts up to date on all of your devices, including your Apple Watch. When you add, change, or remove a shortcut from one of your devices, iCloud ensures that the changes are reflected on your other devices automatically.
The order of your shortcuts is also kept updated between all of your devices.
Important: Personal automation shortcuts are disabled by default during sync.
Turn on iCloud Sync
On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings  > Shortcuts.
Turn on iCloud Sync.
