Add automations to Shortcuts on iPhone or iPad
You can add automations to shortcuts so they run automatically when a trigger, such a specific time of day or a notification is received.
Add an automation trigger to a shortcut
In the Shortcuts app  on your iPhone or iPad, do one the following:
Tap an existing shortcut or create a new shortcut.
Tap Edit, then Automation. Choose a trigger, such as Time of Day or Arrive.
To allow a shortcut to run without user interaction, see Enable an automation to run without asking, below.
Remove an automation trigger from a shortcut
You can remove the automation trigger from your shortcut so it no longer runs automatically.
In the Shortcuts app  on your iPhone or iPad, tap an existing shortcut.
Choose your automation trigger, such as Time of Day or Arrive, and remove it.
Filter shortcuts with automations
You can view a list of your enabled and disabled shortcuts that include an automation.
In the Shortcuts app  on your iPhone or iPad, tap Automation.
Note: For iPhone, you may have to tap  [IMAGE: the Back button]  first.
To filter by Turned On or Turned Off, Tap  [IMAGE: the Filter button] .
Enable an automation to run without asking
Some personal automations can run without asking you for confirmation when they’re triggered.
In the Shortcuts app  on your iPhone or iPad, tap an existing shortcut.
Tap Edit, then  [IMAGE: the Info button] .
Tap Privacy then enable Allow Running When Locked.
The automation will not notify you when it’s triggered.
The following automations can be run automatically:
Time of Day
Alarm
Sleep
Arrive
Leave
CarPlay
Email
Message
Transaction
Wi-Fi
Bluetooth
Apple Watch Workout
NFC
App
Airplane Mode
Do Not Disturb
Low Power Mode
Battery Level
Charger
Sound Recognition
Note: You also may need to set individual actions to run automatically.
