Tap the Hide Toolbar button to collapse it from view. Writing tools There’s a wide range of writing tools to choose from when creating content on your reMarkable. To access the tools and adjust the color or stroke thickness, tap the writing tools icon in the Toolbar . In the Toolbar , you’ll see two writing tools. Tap these to quickly switch between pen styles. With reMarkable Paper Pro and Paper Pro Move, you can write and sketch in color. For reMarkable 1 and 2, you can choose between black, white, and gray. Colors will be available when exporting documents to our apps. Erase, Undo, and Redo Make Edits or Remove Content Tap the Toolbar . Select the Eraser , or use: Undo to reverse your last action. Redo to re-apply an undone action. Read more about the eraser tools . Move, scale, and copy content Use the Selection tool Tap the Selection tool from the toolbar. Draw around the content you’d like to select or draw a horizontal line, hold, and tap Select below . Use the available options to: Move Scale Rotate Copy and paste Read more about the Selection tool Toolbar position Change the Toolbar position In a document Drag the hide/show Toolbar button to reposition the Toolbar according to your preferences. In Settings Go to Settings > Accessibility to choose your preferred Toolbar position. Some of the tools will be moved into Document settings depending on your Toolbar position. Use Layers Create complex sketches or drawings Tap the Layers icon in the Toolbar , or under Document settings . Add, hide, or delete Layers for advanced control of your notes or artwork. Read more about Layers Page overview Tap Page overview from the Toolbar or under Document settings. From here, you can: View all pages in a document Convert handwriting to text Add or delete pages Share pages via email Document settings Adjust how you work Tap Document settings at the bottom of the Toolbar . From here, you can: Access editing tools depending on Toolbar placement Convert handwriting to text Enable Shapes Apply or remove Tags Search the document Share Present with Screen share Send to Slack Send by email Create a link Left- or right-handed mode Optimize pen behavior Go to Settings > Accessibility . Select either Left-handed or Right-handed mode.
