Create PDFs by converting photos from your smartphone.
What you need to get started
Your paper tablet
A smartphone with the reMarkable mobile app
Access to Wi-Fi
How to use the document scanner
In the mobile app, tap Create new.
Tap Scanned document.
Hover over the document you’d like to scan.
Once you see a blue light, the document has been scanned, and you have the option to save it.
Choose to add additional scans to your PDF, or navigate to My files to find your PDF.
Filtering options
After performing a scan, you can add filters to an image before importing it as a PDF to your account. Applying these filters correctly affects how the document appears on your device; we recommend that you avoid filters on pages containing mostly images. The black and white filter is optimal for pages containing mostly text.
