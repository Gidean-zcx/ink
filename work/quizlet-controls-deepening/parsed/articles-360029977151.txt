# Creating sets by importing content

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

Quickly create new flashcard sets based on existing notes or documents by importing them right into Quizlet.

To import your content

  1. Open or create a word or spreadsheet document.
  2. Separate terms and definitions with a comma, tab, or dash.
  3. Separate rows with a semicolon or a new line.
Each row of your document will become a distinct card.

Create a set
  1. 【9†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Select Create.
  3. Select Flashcard set.
  4. Enter a title for your set.

Import your text

  1. Select Import.
  2. Open your document on your computer, and copy the text.
  3. Paste the text into the Import field in your browser.

Save and publish your set

  1. Select Import to upload your terms and definitions.
  2. Set the language for your terms and definitions.
  3. Select Create to save and publish your set.

Settings and options
【10†Quizlet Plus†quizlet.com】 and 【11†Quizlet Plus for teachers†quizlet.com】 subscribers can highlight, bold, italicize, or underline text in sets.

Tips

If you have study notes—not a list of terms and definitions—try 【12†creating a study guide】. This feature is powered by AI and generates questions and answers from longer blocks of notes.

You can currently only import flashcard sets on the website. You can always import content on the Quizlet website and then study it using your iOS app.
You can currently only import flashcard sets on the website. You can always import content on the Quizlet website and then study it using your Android app.
