# Studying with Study Guides

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

Upload or paste your course materials to generate a study guide equipped with an outline, flashcard set, and more with just the click of a button. You can edit and tailor your study guide to fit your study needs. This AI-powered feature makes it easy to tap into the knowledge you create from your own class notes and dive even deeper.

Study guides are available for users who use Quizlet in English, French, or German and are:
  * 14 years or older in the United Kingdom and the United States
  * 15 years or older in Australia
  * 16 years or older in Canada, Ireland, and New Zealand
  * 18 years or older in Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, India, Israel, Malaysia, the Netherlands, Norway, the Philippines, Singapore, South Africa, Sweden, and U.S. territories

Free users can generate study guides for free with limited access. Quizlet Plus and Unlimited Subscribers have unlimited access.

To create a study guide
  1. 【9†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Select Generate.
  3. Select Study guide.
  4. Upload your notes by pasting them in or uploading a file.
  5. Select Start transforming.
  6. Start studying!

Settings and options

  * Select 【10†Image: Size=Small (16)@3x.png】(More) to edit your study guide’s title, change who can see your study guide, view your original upload, or delete your study guide.
  * For generated flashcard sets, go to the set to 【11†change the set’s visibility】.

Tips
  * Find the study guides you created by selecting Your library and Study guides.
  * Select 【12†Image: Share - Resized.png】 (share) to share your study guide.
