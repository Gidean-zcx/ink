# Studying with Practice Tests

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

Practice Tests generate questions based on your uploaded notes or flashcard sets. Different from 【9†Test】, Practice Tests are powered by AI and transform your study materials into full-length practice tests that simulate a real exam. You can choose the amount of questions you want, the question type, and the time limit. Practice Tests are currently available for study material in the sciences and humanities.
【10†Quizlet Plus†quizlet.com】 and 【11†Quizlet Plus for teachers†quizlet.com】 subscribers with English as their selected language can take practice tests.* Practice test generation is available to subscribers who use Quizlet in English and are:
  * 14 years or older in the United States
  * 16 years or older in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand and the United Kingdom
  * 18 years or older in Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, India, Israel, Malaysia, the Netherlands, Norway, the Philippines, Singapore, Samoa, South Africa, Sweden and U.S. territories

If you’re not a Quizlet subscriber, you can use Practice Tests for free with limited access.

To generate and take a practice test
  1. 【12†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Select Generate.
  3. Select Practice test.
  4. Choose flashcard sets or upload your notes by pasting them in or uploading a file.
  5. Select Start transforming.
  6. Adjust the amount of questions, question type, and timer if needed.
  7. Select Take this test.
  8. Take your test.
  9. Select Submit test to see your score and review your test.

Settings and options
  * Select 【13†Image: Share - Resized.png】 (share) to share your practice test.
  * Select 【14†Image: Size=Small (16)@3x.png】 (more) to change your practice test's visibility, edit the title, or see your original upload.

*【15†Younger users】 can take multiple choice practice tests and will need to have a parent confirm their account to access written questions.

To study with Practice tests
  1. Select +.
  2. Select Practice test.
  3. Upload your notes by pasting text or select a file.
  4. Select the number of questions.
  5. Choose multiple-choice or written question types.
  6. Select Take this test.

Tips
  * Find your saved Practice tests in Your library under Practice tests.
  * When pasting text, ensure it contains at least 300 characters. Written responses cannot exceed 200 characters
  * Currently, selecting specific sets to convert into practice tests and adding practice tests to folders are only available on the web but will be added to mobile in a future update.
