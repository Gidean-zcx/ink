# Studying most-missed terms with stars

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

As you study, you can star the terms you want to focus on later to make the best use of your study time.

To star terms from a set page

  1. 【9†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Open a set.
  3. Select the Star on terms you want to focus on later.
  4. Select the Starred button to study only starred terms.
  5. Select Review with an activity to start a session with only starred terms.
Other people can't see the terms you've starred.

Settings and options

  * To star a group of terms all at once, hold the Shift key as you select a star. Keep holding Shift until you select the star of the last term you want to include.
  * To study all starred terms across all sets at once, you can add all the sets to a folder. Then, select an activity, select Options and select Study starred.
