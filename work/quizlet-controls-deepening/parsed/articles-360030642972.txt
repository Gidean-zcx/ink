# Studying with Test

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

Test gives you the chance to see how you'll perform on an exam.

Test is available for 【9†Quizlet Plus†quizlet.com】 and 【10†Quizlet Plus for teachers†quizlet.com】 subscribers. If you're not a Quizlet subscriber, you can study one free practice test per flashcard set in Test.

To study with Test
  1. 【11†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Open a set.
  3. Select Test.
  4. Set the number and types of questions you'd like to include.
  5. Select Start test.
  6. Take your test.
  7. Select Submit test to see your score and review your test.

To share your graded test or keep a copy for yourself, select Print test.

Tips
  * Test doesn't save progress like other activities. If you navigate away from your test before you're done, your progress isn't saved.
  * To test yourself only on terms you've starred, select Study only starred terms from Options.
  * Teachers can 【12†create classes】 and 【13†add flashcard sets】 to those classes. Any class members 【14†invited to the class】 will then have free access to Learn and Test for the flashcard sets in that class.
