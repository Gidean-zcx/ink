# Editing sets

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

You can easily edit your flashcard sets. You can also 【9†copy someone else's flashcard set】 and then edit your version.*

To edit your set

  1. 【10†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Open the set.
  3. Select 【11†Image: More_menu_-_iOS_-_Resized.png】  (more menu).
  4. Select 【12†Image: Edit_-_Resized.png】 (edit).
  5. Make your changes and select Done.

To change the audio playback language
  1. 【10†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Open the set.
  3. Select 【13†Image: More_menu_-_iOS_-_Resized.png】 (more menu).
  4. Select 【14†Image: Edit_-_Resized.png】 (edit).
  5. Click on the text of a term or definition.
  6. Select the current language.
  7. Choose a new language from the menu.
  8. Select Done.

To rearrange terms and definitions
  1. 【10†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Open the set.
  3. Select 【15†Image: More_menu_-_iOS_-_Resized.png】 (more menu).
  4. Select 【16†Image: Edit_-_Resized.png】 (edit).
  5. Hover over a row until your pointer becomes crossed arrows.
  6. Click and drag the row to where you want it.

To switch terms and definitions between columns, select the double-sided arrow.

To delete terms and definitions

Select 【17†Image: Trash_-_Resized.png】 (trash can) on the row you'd like to delete.
Settings and options

Subscribers to 【18†Quizlet Plus†quizlet.com】 and 【19†Quizlet Plus for teachers†quizlet.com】 can highlight, bold, italicize, or underline text in sets.

Tips

To insert a row in the middle of your set, hover your pointer between two rows and select the +.

To change the audio playback language

  1. Select the text of a term or definition.
  2. Select the current language.
