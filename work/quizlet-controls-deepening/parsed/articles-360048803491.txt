# Using Progress for targeted studying

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

Progress is available in 【9†Quizlet Plus subscriptions†quizlet.com】.

Progress tracks your answers across activities to give you a comprehensive view of your study activity. You'll see a set's terms grouped according to how often you answered them correctly or incorrectly. Progress is not tracked on game modes. Your study progress syncs across the website and Quizlet mobile apps so you can keep studying, no matter where you are.
To use Progress for targeted studying

  1. 【10†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Open a set.
  3. Star Not Studied, Still Learning, or Mastered under the set preview and select Starred.
  4. Select Review with an activity to start a session with only 【11†starred terms】.

  1. Open a set.
  2. Select Not Studied, Still Learning, or Mastered to start studying that specific group of terms.
