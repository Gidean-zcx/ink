# Removing sets from your Recent activity feed

【8†IOS】 【8†Web】 【8†Android】

Your Recent activity feed shows sets that have been added to your classes or that you have studied recently.

To remove a set from your Recent activity feed
  1. 【9†Log in†quizlet.com】 to your account.
  2. Select Your library.
  3. Select Flashcard sets.
  4. Select Studied from the dropdown menu.
  5. Select 【10†Image: More_menu_-_iOS_-_Resized.png】 (More menu) by the title of the set you want to hide.
  6. Select Remove.
  7. Select Hide this set.

You can’t remove sets from your feed on the iOS app. Instead, you can log in to your account from your mobile browser app and then follow 【11†these instructions】 for removing sets on the website.
You can’t remove sets from your feed on the Android app. Instead, you can log in to your account from your mobile browser app and then follow 【11†these instructions】 for removing sets on the website.
