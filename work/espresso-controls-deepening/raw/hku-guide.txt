Overview of Espresso from The Economist
What is Espresso?
Espresso is a daily news briefing service from The Economist, providing concise summaries of important global events and trends. It is available via mobile App. Currently, it is a service completely FREE to students worldwide.
Purpose of the Service
To keep users informed with quick, digestible content that highlights essential news stories and insights.
Key Features
Daily access to five brief articles.
Weekly reads featuring four selected full articles from The Economist magazine, accompanied by a podcast.
Short videos (under four minutes) covering various topics.
Articles are carefully selected by The Economist’s editors to ensure relevance and quality.
